# DEPLOYMENT/what_this_system_is_not.md — honest limits (P19, Rule 19)

PLAIN STATEMENT of what the system must NOT be relied upon for, and the preconditions for any real-world
reliance. This is binding (Rule 19).

## What this system is NOT
- NOT a certified fire-safety device. It does not replace certified fire-detection/alarm panels, sprinklers,
  or statutory fire-safety measures.
- NOT an autonomous alerting or evacuation system. It never triggers public alerts or evacuation on its own.
- NOT an official alerting channel. It may not originate official public warnings without authority.
- NOT a surveillance / person-identification / tracking tool. No biometric or individual-tracking features.
- NOT validated. No performance claim about OUR system exists; all cited numbers are other papers' source-
  reported results on other data (Rule 16).
- NOT proven in India. India context, standards, costs, and impact are unverified research notes pending
  official verification and professional review.
- NOT a disaster "solution". It is at most a complementary, advisory decision-support aid (1912.04883).

## What it CAN be (research-phase)
A research design + literature-grounded plan for an offline-first, uncertainty-aware, human-in-the-loop,
multi-sensor + CCTV early-warning aid that presents itself as COMPLEMENTARY until certified.

## Preconditions BEFORE any real-world reliance
1. Independent validation on leakage-controlled, cross-site/cross-season data with honest statistics.
2. Independent expert review (fire science incl. physics Class A/B/C, ML, safety, legal/standards, ethics).
3. Certification as required for a safety device; official approval for any alerting role.
4. Robustness + security evidence (P16) and demonstrated fail-safe to "uncertain/offline", never a false
   "all clear".
5. Data-protection / privacy / surveillance-law compliance verified by qualified professionals.
6. A successful staged pilot (shadow → advisory) with pre-registered success metrics and stopping rules.

Until ALL preconditions are met, outputs are advisory-only and a human authority remains decisive.
