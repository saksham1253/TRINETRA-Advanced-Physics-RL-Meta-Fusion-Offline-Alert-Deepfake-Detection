## Content (own words — abstract only)
- Problem: remotely-sensed precipitation products (e.g. IMERG) have a few hours of LATENCY; a nowcasting
  system can cut that latency and add warning value for FLASH FLOODS and LANDSLIDES. Hard because the
  atmosphere is chaotic and precip structures change fast.
- Method: "Nowcasting-Nets" — Recurrent + Convolutional DNN structures; FIVE models trained on GPM
  **IMERG** precipitation over Eastern CONUS, tested on independent Eastern + Western CONUS.
  Lead time up to 1.5 h, extended to 4.5 h via a feedback-loop approach.
- Baselines (THE KEY POINT FOR US): compared against Random Forest, Linear Regression, AND a
  **PERSISTENCE benchmark (most-recent-observation-as-forecast)**.
- Headline result (ABSTRACT, UNVERIFIED — D-008): the Convolutional Nowcasting Network **with a
  RESIDUAL HEAD (CNC-R)** achieves 25%, 28%, 46% improvement in the respective tests (abstract text
  truncated before full metric definition — do NOT over-read these numbers).
