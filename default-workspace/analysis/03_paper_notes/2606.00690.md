## What it is
A **sequential conformal-prediction (CP)** method that keeps coverage valid when the exchangeability
assumption breaks — which it does on real time series (temporal dependence + distribution shift).
- **Patching:** slide a window over residuals ε to form patches that **capture local temporal
  dependence** → yields "approximate LOCAL exchangeability" rather than assuming global exchangeability.
- **Matching tree:** builds a binary tree that groups **similar residual patches** (label-free gain
  score based on distribution matching, Kolmogorov–Smirnov statistic) → adaptive BINS of
  exchangeable-enough residuals.
- Per-bin **quantile-regression** models produce intervals; inference routes a new patch to its bin.
- **Theory:** approximate-local-exchangeability, matching-tree convergence, a **coverage guarantee via
  distributional stability**, plus bootstrapping + online updating for robustness.

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (WS8 UQ layer; resolves CR-04)
- **CR-04 was: is naive conformal valid on TRINETRA's streams?** Answer sharpened: naive CP assumes
  exchangeability, which our autocorrelated, regime-shifting sensor/InSAR streams violate (2303.12783,
  2511.11567). DistMatch gives a **principled, theory-backed route**: don't assume global
  exchangeability — **find locally-exchangeable bins via residual-distribution matching, then conform
  within bins**, with online updating for drift.
- Complements the two CP anchors already in the corpus: **EnbPI** (2010.09107, ensemble OOB residuals,
  the baseline per-zone method) and **PID-Expert** (2610.02777 P2, online width-efficiency aggregation).
  → WS8 conformal design = EnbPI/DistMatch base for validity-under-dependence + PID-Expert-style online
  width control + per-zone Mondrian stratification. DistMatch's "adaptive binning" IS a data-driven way
  to form the Mondrian strata we hand-designed per zone.
- Directly disciplines the report's old "conformal guarantee" overclaim (Bug #13): coverage is
  **approximate under a distributional-stability condition**, not hard — exactly the honest framing we
  must carry (ties E-07 cost-loss + the calibrated-uncertainty process target).
