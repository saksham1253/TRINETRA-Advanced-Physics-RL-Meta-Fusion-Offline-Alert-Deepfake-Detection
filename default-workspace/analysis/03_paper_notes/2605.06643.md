## What it is
A large, **fair-comparison benchmark (MMDG-Bench)** asking whether reported gains in Multimodal Domain
Generalization are REAL algorithmic progress or artifacts of inconsistent protocols. Standardizes
evaluation across **6 datasets × 3 tasks (action recognition, mechanical fault diagnosis, sentiment
analysis), 6 modality combinations, 9 methods**, with **7,402 networks trained across 95 cross-domain
tasks**, assessing not just accuracy but corruption robustness, missing-modality generalization,
misclassification detection, and OOD detection.

## Five findings (as published) — a bundle of our core theses
1. **Under FAIR comparison, specialized MMDG methods give only MARGINAL improvement over the ERM
   baseline.** (Confirmed in the table I read: ERM avg 68.11 vs best specialized MOOSA 71.29 — a few
   points.) → direct CR-02 corroboration (simple baseline nearly matches complex methods).
2. **No single method consistently outperforms** across datasets or modality combinations. → ties
   2606.13896 (rankings flip by task) and RB on not trusting a single leaderboard winner.
3. **A substantial gap to the upper bound (oracle) persists** — MMDG is far from solved.
4. **Trimodal fusion does NOT consistently beat the strongest bimodal config** — more modalities ≠
   better (directly relevant: our sat+CCTV+IoT fusion is not automatically superior).
5. **ALL methods degrade significantly under corruption and missing modalities; SOME further
   COMPROMISE trustworthiness** (worse calibration/OOD when inputs are degraded). → CR-07 / FM-15.

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (CR-02 + CR-07 + eval harness + fusion doctrine)
- **Single most consolidating eval-critique for our multimodal design.** It simultaneously supports:
  (a) CR-02 parsimony — ERM ≈ specialized methods under fair comparison → always run a plain ERM/
  concatenation-fusion baseline; (b) CR-07/FM-15 — fusion is NOT robust to missing/corrupted modalities
  for free, and robustness interventions can HURT trustworthiness; (c) the "more modalities ≠ better"
  caution against assuming our 3-layer fusion dominates a well-tuned 2-layer one.
- **Eval-harness mandates for TRINETRA (add to WS8/eval spec):**
  - Report under a STANDARDIZED protocol with fixed splits; include ERM baseline + oracle upper bound
    to show the real gap.
  - Evaluate the SAME model under clean / corrupted / each-missing-modality, and report calibration +
    OOD-detection in EACH condition (trustworthiness must not silently degrade when a layer drops).
  - Test ranking STABILITY (their Appendix B) — don't crown a method on one dataset.
- Pairs with Admissable (2609.15297, worst-case missingness) and 2310.03986 (missing-modality
  adaptation): MMDG-Bench shows the problem is pervasive and benchmarked; Admissable gives the correct
  (adversarial) threat model; 2310.03986 gives a cheap fix. Together → the missing-modality engineering
  package for the build.
