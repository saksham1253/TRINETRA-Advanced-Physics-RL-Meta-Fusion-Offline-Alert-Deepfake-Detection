## What it is
DL **surrogates for a physics-based fire-spread simulator** (Rothermel CA / FARSITE / FlamMap),
trained on **10,584 sims at 2 m** for Rectoret, Catalonia. Four architectures: patch U-Net,
transfer ResNet-50, a **PINN constrained by the wind-driven advection equation**, and Swin-Unet.
Interpretability via saliency + occlusion + rotation; cross-region test on Pedriza (no retraining).

## Key findings (as-published)
- **Feature parsimony:** of all terrain/vegetation variables, **only surface fuel load** predicts
  burn probability with any strength (r=0.27); adding it lowers error 21%. The rest are weak and
  "highly duplicative." → most inputs are redundant.
- **What the models actually use:** convolutional models rely primarily on **distance from the
  current fire front** (geometry), while Swin-Unet weights fuel/terrain more. Occlusion: removing
  the distance channel doubles fire-mask importance (0.121→0.248) while the 8 environmental channels
  stay flat (0.399→0.342) — the model just re-encodes the SAME geometric information.
- Terrain+fuel+wind alone still reach MCC 0.42 → environmental signal is real but **second-order**;
  near-term spread is dominated by fire-front geometry.
- **Cross-region transfer:** all three convolutional models still predict on Pedriza without
  retraining, losing accuracy by "a small but systematic margin."

## Why it matters to TRINETRA
- **CR-02 (simplicity dominates) — FIFTH domain datapoint (wildfire spread).** The predictive signal
  is overwhelmingly a simple geometric quantity (distance to current front) + ONE physical driver
  (fuel load); the fancy transformer's extra "environmental attention" doesn't translate to a
  different answer. Reinforces: start from the simplest sufficient predictor, add complexity only if
  it beats it under honest CV.
- **Interpretability method is reusable:** occlusion/saliency showing "the model re-encodes the same
  geometry" is exactly the audit we should run on our anomaly/physics-gate features (Rule 19
  auditability) — catches models that look environment-aware but are really using one proxy.
- **Cross-region "small but systematic" degradation** is the realistic transfer story for our
  Uttarakhand↔Himachal generalization: expect a consistent accuracy drop out-of-region; budget for
  it and test spatial CV (our process target).
- PINN-with-advection-constraint is a candidate design pattern if TRINETRA ever adds a fire/plume
  module (not core; our hazards are landslide/flood/GLOF).
