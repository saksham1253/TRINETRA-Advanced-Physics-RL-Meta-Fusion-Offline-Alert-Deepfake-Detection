## Content (own words)
- Thesis: pure physics models are rigid (unrealistic params sometimes); pure ML ignores physical
  constraints + lacks interpretability and suffers EQUIFINALITY (different structures/params →
  same accuracy). Note the sharp caution: "scientific knowledge that does not add to predictive
  ability may be deceptive" — i.e. don't bolt on physics for its own sake. Need a SYNERGISTIC
  hybrid.
- Method: PIML combines a CONCEPTUAL hydrological model (the "abcd" model) with SOTA ML.
  Architecture variants also do UNCERTAINTY QUANTIFICATION on target (streamflow Q) and
  intermediate (actual evapotranspiration ET) variables.
- Application: monthly streamflow + ET in the NARMADA river basin, INDIA. Results: PIML
  outperforms the purely conceptual abcd model AND plain ML, while ensuring PHYSICAL CONSISTENCY
  verified via annual WATER-BALANCE analysis. Verified Table 2 (abcd baseline, validation
  2009-2014, streamflow Q NSE): target=Q → NSE 0.815; target=Q+ET → NSE 0.827; calibrating
  against more variables (Q,ET,SM,GW) degraded ET RMSE (60.9) — more constraints ≠ always better.
