## Content (own words — abstract only)
- Problem: GLOF forecasting is hindered by fragmented, unimodal, mostly post-event data.
- Contribution: GLOFNet, a public MULTIMODAL dataset for the Shisper Glacier (Karakoram),
  integrating (1) Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery, (2) NASA ITS_LIVE glacier-velocity
  products, (3) MODIS Land Surface Temperature over 20+ years. Preprocessing: cloud masking,
  quality filtering, normalization, temporal interpolation, augmentation, cyclical encoding,
  cross-modal harmonization. EDA reports seasonal velocity cycles, ~0.8 K/decade warming,
  spatial heterogeneity. Positioned as a benchmark base for multimodal DL on rare-hazard
  prediction; explicitly tackles class imbalance, cloud contamination, coarse resolution.
