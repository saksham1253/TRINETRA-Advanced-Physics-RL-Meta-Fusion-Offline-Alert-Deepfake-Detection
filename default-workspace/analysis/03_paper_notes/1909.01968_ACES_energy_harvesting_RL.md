## Content (own words)
- Problem: wireless sensors mostly battery-powered → periodic battery replacement blocks large
  deployments. Energy-HARVESTING sensors avoid batteries but must deliver adequate Quality of
  Service (QoS) under UNCERTAIN energy availability.
- Method: ACES uses REINFORCEMENT LEARNING (Q-learning) to maximize sensing quality of
  energy-harvesting sensors for periodic + event-driven sensing within the available energy
  budget. Platform: supercapacitor storage + Bluetooth Low Energy. Uses day-by-day CONTINUAL
  learning (adapts to changing environment, no separate data-collection phase) + TRANSFER
  LEARNING to cut deployment training time.
- Verified results (abstract): 60-node deployment, 2 weeks → nodes idle only 0.1% of time;
  data collection comparable to battery-powered nodes; ACES cuts node duty-cycle period by avg
  33% vs three prior RL techniques, while continually learning.
- Verified hyperparameters (Table 3, Q-learning): γ=0.99, ε_max=1, ε_min=0.1, ε-increment
  0.0004, α=0.1, episode=24 h, wait time T=15 min, training phase=15 days.
