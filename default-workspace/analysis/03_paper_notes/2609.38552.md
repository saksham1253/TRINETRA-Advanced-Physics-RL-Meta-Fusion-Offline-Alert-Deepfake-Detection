## What it is
A study of **Early Warning Systems that institutions procure but cannot inspect or change**
(proprietary higher-ed student-risk EWS). When you can't touch the model, the only lever is
**adjusting model OUTPUTS** post-hoc. The paper evaluates whether that post-hoc fairness fixing
actually works — and names the failure "**fairness theatre**": interventions that LOOK like they
address inequity but don't meaningfully change outcomes.
- Frames the **ASP–HEI cycle** (AI Solution Provider ↔ Higher-Ed Institution) and the constrained
  **intervention space** available to a buyer of a black-box EWS.
- Catalogues post-hoc techniques (reject-option, selective prediction-flipping to meet equalized-odds
  with an accuracy floor, group-disparity bias-mitigation post-processing) and their REQUIREMENTS
  (most need group labels + observed outcomes at configuration time) and limits (results hinge on the
  chosen fairness constraint + accuracy floor).

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (governance, procurement, bias-fairness, Rule 19)
- **TRINETRA will issue alerts that differentially affect communities** (which valleys get warned,
  which get evacuated). If any component is a procured black box (a vendor EO model, a commercial
  nowcast), the "fairness theatre" warning applies directly: **output-only tweaks can't fix an
  inequitable upstream model**, and may give false assurance.
- **Design mandates:**
  1. Keep TRINETRA's decision stack **inspectable/open** where it affects who gets warned — don't rely
     on post-hoc output adjustment to achieve equity (interpretability already favored: 2610.03380,
     2609.18555, 2608.16654).
  2. **Fairness must be evaluated at the DECISION/outcome level per region/community**, not just
     aggregate accuracy — report per-zone miss-rate and alert-rate disparities (ties the per-zone
     Mondrian-conformal design and the cost-loss framing).
  3. Procurement caution for the BOM/vendor phase (P6): prefer components we can audit; document the
     intervention space we actually have over any closed component.
- **Honest-governance anchor** for the ethics/▲-for-expert-review notes: fairness isn't a checkbox
  applied at the end; it's a property of the whole pipeline + the procurement relationship.
