## Problem & why it matters
DIRECTLY informs CR-01 (which EO foundation-model encoder to pick). Studies SIX representative GeoFMs
across joint-embedding / reconstruction / multimodal pretraining families and asks how they transfer
to classification, regression, and segmentation — including Sen1Floods11 (flood) and PASTIS. The
headline: GeoFM RANKINGS are NOT stable — they flip with task type and pretraining objective. This
is exactly the evidence base our CR-01 four-contender trade study (DINOv2/CROMA/Copernicus-FM/AnySat)
needs, and it warns against picking an encoder by leaderboard.

## Core idea / method (reusable)
- Evaluate 6 GeoFMs on multiple downstream tasks + benchmarks (PANGAEA segmentation, NeuCo
  regression, EuroSAT kNN, Sen1Floods11, PASTIS).
- Layerwise LINEAR PROBING to see where useful representations emerge across ViT depth.
- CKA (centered kernel alignment) same-layer comparison pretrained vs fine-tuned → measures how much
  representations SHIFT during adaptation (2,500 deterministic images from PASTIS / Sen1Floods11).

## Key results (as stated; big table pruned)
- GeoFM rankings are SENSITIVE to task type: no single model wins across classification / regression /
  segmentation. Pretraining OBJECTIVE strongly affects which downstream task a model is good at.
- Layerwise probing: best-transferring representations are not always the final layer → mid-block
  features can be better for some tasks.
- Fine-tuning shifts representations measurably (CKA) — the amount of shift varies by model/task.

## Assumptions & limitations
- Six models only; benchmarks are standard EO (not Himalayan hazard). Author list not captured
  (backfill). Linear-probe / CKA are proxies for "transfer".
