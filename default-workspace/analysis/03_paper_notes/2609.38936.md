## Problem & why it matters
When you can only spare a FEW THOUSAND held-out examples for post-hoc calibration (our exact
situation — rare hazard labels), the CAPACITY of the calibration map is a statistical design choice:
a scalar temperature UNDERFITS structured residual miscalibration, but a highly adaptive calibrator
cannot be estimated reliably from so little data. Directly bears on TRINETRA's small-validation
regime and reinforces that temperature scaling is NOT a universal fix (FM-20, with 2306.04590).

## Core idea / method (re-implementable sketch)
- SRTS-BCE: a 10-PARAMETER, argmax-preserving calibrator.
- Cross-fit a correctness-RISK score from SIX logit statistics (e.g. max logit, margin, entropy…),
  then fit ONE top-label-BCE temperature per K=3 RISK GROUPS (risk-conditioned / "signal-routed").
- Recovers standard TvA temperature scaling as the K=1 limit → principled interpolation between
  scalar and high-capacity calibration.
- Theory: fixed-partition second-order BCE expansion — grouped-over-pooled test-BCE gain depends on
  between-group variance B of the calibration gradient vs within-group variance; B=0 ⇒ no benefit
  (tells you WHEN routing helps).

## Data / splits
- Fine-tuned CIFAR-100 / ViT-B/16; Tiny-ImageNet; multiple backbones (ResNet, Swin-T). Budgets
  swept down to n=250 calibration examples.

## Key results (as stated)
- At full budget: SRTS-BCE cuts ECE15 from 1.65 (scalar TvA-TS) to 0.96, MATCHING a higher-capacity
  head (SMART+BCE 0.95) — i.e. low-capacity routing achieves high-capacity calibration quality.
- As budget SHRINKS the regimes separate: at n=250 SRTS-BCE BEATS the high-capacity head on all
  three CIFAR-100 backbones. Capacity is a FINITE-SAMPLE design choice whose best level grows with
  available calibration data.
- Budget-dependent ranking REVERSAL on Swin-T (Tiny-ImageNet) — more calibration-capacity can HURT
  at small budgets. Ablation: online controller + conditional-scale normalization give most of the gain.

## Assumptions & limitations
- Classification calibration (not regression/TS). Objective is top-label BCE, not ECE directly
  (authors note + Brier cross-check). Preprint.
