## What it is
A **label-free PINN** for non-associated Mohr-Coulomb plasticity with **incremental strength
reduction** to compute the factor of safety (FoS) of **spatially variable c–φ slopes**, trained with
a **virtual-work (weak-form) loss on the FE mesh**, solving many random fields at once on a GPU. A
**neural operator** trained ONLY on PINN solutions then predicts FoS, displacement path and failure
mode for a **million random fields in seconds**. No finite-element solution enters the pipeline;
Abaqus is used only to VERIFY.

## Key technical facts (as-published)
- Strength Reduction Method (SRM): reduce strength by factor s: c_s = c/s, tan φ_s = tan φ / s;
  increase s until equilibrium fails; FoS F = critical s. (Eq. 9 in the paper — read directly.)
- Hard boundary conditions baked into the displacement ansatz; weak-form/virtual-work loss (not
  strong-form PDE residual) — this is the "label-free" physics supervision.
- Accuracy: reproduces Abaqus FoS within **2–3% RMS over 52 random fields**; locates the failure
  mechanism in the same weak zones (plastic strain band under-resolved).
- Reliability: Monte Carlo + FORM + importance sampling + the operator give **consistent failure
  probabilities down to 1e−3**. BUT a 3% FoS scatter **inflates a 1e−3 probability by up to 2×** —
  tail sensitivity. A heterogeneous Bishop analysis gives failure probs 2.6–8.3× LOWER (small FoS
  differences amplified in the tail).

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (WS1 physics gate)
- **Directly relevant to our FS=Ff+Fw+Fc / infinite-slope / TRIGRS physics gate.** This is a modern,
  GPU-scalable way to compute FoS over SPATIALLY VARIABLE strength fields — more realistic than a
  single-column infinite-slope FS. Candidate upgrade path for the physics gate in later build phases
  (research-design note only now).
- **Tail-sensitivity warning is the key takeaway:** a 3% error in FoS → up to 2× error in a 1e−3
  failure probability; different methods disagree 2.6–8.3×. For a life-safety gate (Rule 19) this
  means **FoS-derived probabilities are NOT trustworthy at the tail** — use FoS as a GATE/threshold
  (FS<1 → escalate), not as a calibrated probability. Reinforces our existing design: physics gate
  is a hard override, not a probability source. Add to WS1 limitations + FM catalogue.
- Label-free (no FE labels needed) + operator surrogate (million fields in seconds) is attractive
  under our Kaggle compute cap — but it's a BUILD-phase option, not a research-only action now.
