## Content (own words, from abstract)
- Problem: tailings dams impound saturated soil highly susceptible to LIQUEFACTION; if the
  dam breaches, a massive debris flow of liquefied soil is released with disastrous downstream
  consequences. Numerical models inform hazard-avoidance/mitigation engineering decisions.
- Core idea: FEM is good at liquefaction TRIGGERING + initial movement but loses accuracy at
  large deformation (mesh distortion); MPM (hybrid Eulerian-Lagrangian, particles on a
  background grid) handles large deformation but has stress-accuracy + boundary-condition
  limits that impair modeling liquefaction INITIATION. Solution: a SEQUENTIAL HYBRIDIZATION —
  FEM for initiation, hand off to MPM for runout — superior to either alone. Demonstrated on
  the 1978 Mochikoshi Tailings Dam failure (initiation → runout).
