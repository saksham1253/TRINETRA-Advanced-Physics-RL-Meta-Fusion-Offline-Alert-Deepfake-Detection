## Content (own words)
- Problem: EO data varies hugely in modality, scale, and spatial/temporal/spectral resolution;
  existing EO foundation models expect FIXED input configs and can't ingest new dataset formats
  without retraining from scratch — defeating the "foundation model" purpose.
- Method: AnySat — a multimodal model on JOINT EMBEDDING PREDICTIVE ARCHITECTURE (JEPA) +
  SCALE-ADAPTIVE spatial encoders, trained self-supervised on highly heterogeneous data. Can
  handle resolutions from ~1.25 m to 1280 m tiles and sensors incl. aerial, SPOT6, NAIP,
  Sentinel-1, ALOS-2, Sentinel-2, Landsat 7/8/9.
- Scope of evidence: compiled GeoPlex (55 multimodal datasets, 11 distinct sensors); trained ONE
  model on all simultaneously; reports SOTA (after finetune/probe) on GeoPlex test sets + 6
  external datasets across: land-cover, tree-species, crop-type, change detection, and
  segmentation of FLOOD, BURN SCAR, DEFORESTATION. Code/models at github.com/gastruc/AnySat.

## Relevance to us
- Component: WS8/WS1 EO encoder — FOURTH named CR-01 contender (now: DINOv2 generic / CROMA
  radar-optical / Copernicus-FM any-modality hypernetwork / AnySat JEPA scale-adaptive). AnySat's
  distinguishing strength = NATIVE multi-RESOLUTION + multi-SCALE handling with a single model,
  and it explicitly does FLOOD + BURN-SCAR segmentation (hazard-adjacent to us). The scale-
  adaptive encoder is attractive for TRINETRA because our inputs span very different resolutions
  (S1/S2 10 m, MODIS/GPM coarse, drone/CCTV fine) → one model ingesting all is appealing.
- Design tension: like Copernicus-FM, AnySat is HIGH complexity. CR-02 doctrine (2608.12422:
  simple baselines often win under honest spatial-CV) demands we justify it vs a plain ViT +
  gradient-boosted baseline on OUR tasks before adopting. JEPA (feature-space prediction) is a
  modern SSL objective worth noting alongside MAE/contrastive (CROMA, SSL4EO-S12).
- Honesty caveats: their SOTA is on GeoPlex/external benchmarks, NOT our hazard tasks (Rule 16);
  flood/burn-scar segmentation ≠ landslide/GLOF precursor forecasting. Numbers partially unread.
  confidence: high on relevance + architecture novelty; med on transfer.
