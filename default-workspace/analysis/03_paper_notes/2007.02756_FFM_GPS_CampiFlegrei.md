## Content (own words, from abstract + named method)
- The paper applies the FAILURE FORECAST METHOD (FFM) to present-day unrest at Campi Flegrei
  caldera, using horizontal DEFORMATION data from ELEVEN GPS stations over 2011–2020, to make
  a first assessment of a "failure time."
- Background (established, not from this PDF's interior): FFM / Fukuzono–Voight is the
  1/v = A(α−1)(t_f − t) acceleration-to-failure law (our E-04), where inverse velocity goes
  linearly to zero at failure time t_f for α≈2. That Barry Voight (originator of the material-
  failure relation) is a co-author makes this a primary-lineage FFM application paper.
