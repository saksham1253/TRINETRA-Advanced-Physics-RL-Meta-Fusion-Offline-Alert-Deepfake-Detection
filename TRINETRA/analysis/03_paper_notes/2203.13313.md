## What it is
A benchmark DL study showing that **fault-zone acoustic emission (AE) energy is a FINGERPRINT of fault
shear stress**, and that DL can both (a) PREDICT lab-earthquake timing and (b) AUTOREGRESSIVELY
FORECAST the continuous evolution of fault stress. Lab stick-slip ("labquakes") are the controlled
analog of the seismic cycle.
- **Prediction (instantaneous):** LSTM + CNN models predict labquakes under hard conditions —
  pre-seismic creep, APERIODIC events, alternating slow/fast events; outperform prior ML; work with
  limited training data.
- **Two failure-timing targets:** **TTsF** (time to START of failure) and **TTeF** (time to END of
  failure). KEY RESULT: **TTeF is predicted reliably in ALL seismic cycles, but TTsF accuracy DEGRADES
  with the amount of pre-seismic creep** — i.e. the MORE gradual-creep precursor present, the HARDER
  the onset-time is to pin down.
- **Autoregressive forecasting (novel framing):** iteratively forecasts the FUTURE stress trajectory
  (not just one variable at one time) using three sequence backbones — **LSTM, Temporal Convolution
  Network (TCN), Transformer**. Forecasting BEYOND a single seismic cycle is "limited but encouraging."

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (precursor doctrine, Fukuzono 1/v, AR-forecast-residual anomaly, honest limits)
- **Physical validation of the precursor-energy → stress link** that underpins our WS1 precursor layer.
  AE-energy↔stress is the lab analog of our creep/micro-signal → slope-stress reasoning; it legitimizes
  treating rising precursor energy as a stress proxy — WITH the caveats below.
- **Directly informs the anomaly pipeline's forecast-residual branch.** Our design uses
  forecast-residual as one anomaly channel; this paper is concrete evidence that AR sequence models
  (TCN/Transformer/LSTM) can forecast a geophysical stress signal, and that iterative multi-step AR is
  feasible — supports the "SSL forecast → residual = anomaly" branch (ties WinoTS 2609.39337 temporal
  SSL and the Deep-SVDD/forecast-residual ensemble).
- **TTsF vs TTeF asymmetry = a crucial honest lesson for lead-time claims.** The onset of failure
  (TTsF) — exactly the quantity an EWS most wants — is the HARDEST to predict, and gets HARDER with
  more creep. This directly disciplines our lead-time messaging: **we must NOT promise sharp onset
  prediction; a long gradual-creep precursor can WIDEN onset uncertainty, not narrow it.** Pairs with
  the Fukuzono/Voight 1/v=A(α−1)(t_f−t) framework (Bug #4): 1/v linearization gives a failure-time
  ESTIMATE with wide error bars, and this paper quantifies why onset timing is intrinsically uncertain
  under creep → feeds RB (lead-time honesty) and the conformal interval width on any t_f estimate.
- **Backbone trade:** TCN/Transformer/LSTM all viable for AR geophysical forecasting — a data point for
  our sequence-encoder trade study (alongside the EO-FM encoders), favoring TCN for cheap on-edge AR.

## Caveats / honesty
- LABORATORY stick-slip on a controlled biax apparatus — NOT field earthquakes and NOT landslides;
  the authors are explicit that field generalization is open. Transfer to TRINETRA is at the level of
  PRINCIPLES (AE↔stress fingerprint; AR multi-step forecasting feasible; onset timing intrinsically
  hard under creep), not numbers or models.
- Earthquake, not landslide/flood — but the creep→failure precursor physics is shared with slope creep
  (Fukuzono/Saito), which is why it is directly relevant to our 1/v layer.
- "Forecasting beyond one cycle limited but encouraging" — do not overread multi-cycle AR.
