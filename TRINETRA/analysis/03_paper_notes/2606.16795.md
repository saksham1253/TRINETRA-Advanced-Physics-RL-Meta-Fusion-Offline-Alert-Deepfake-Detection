## What it is
A learning-based pipeline that CORRECTS atmospheric phase artefacts in **unwrapped InSAR
interferograms**, validated against **GNSS** ground truth at two volcanoes (Laguna del Maule, Campi
Flegrei). Architecture components:
- **Wavelet decoder (WaveMamba-style):** low-frequency subband + enhanced high-frequency components,
  recombined via inverse wavelet transform — explicit time-frequency/multi-scale separation.
- **DINOv3 Cross-Attention (DCA) conditioning:** frozen DINOv3 vision-foundation features condition the
  denoiser (semantic/texture context).
- **Terrain-Aware Atmospheric Conditioning (TMB):** DEM elevation + gradients modulate feature maps
  (atmospheric delay is strongly terrain/elevation-correlated).
- **Phase Ramp Head (PRH):** explicitly models large-scale smooth atmospheric artefacts as a first-order
  2D planar ramp (low-order global trend).
- Trained on **synthetic deformation + real atmospheric noise**; cuts GNSS misfit (~3% / 19% at the two
  volcanoes, as noted in prior ABS) — beats numerical-weather-model corrections.

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (the REQUIRED InSAR preprocessing stage our pipeline omitted — input integrity)
- **This is the missing preprocessing stage for TRINETRA's InSAR layer, and it is a known pipeline bug
  (input integrity #1/#3).** Our datacube assumes clean unwrapped phase, but Himalayan
  tropospheric/seasonal delay is SEVERE and, uncorrected, masquerades as deformation → false landslide
  signal into the physics gate. WaveDINO is concrete, GNSS-VALIDATED evidence that a learned correction
  beats weather-model corrections, and gives an architecture to adopt.
- **Perfect complement to Rouet-Leduc #30 (2012.13849):** that paper SEPARATES deformation from
  atmosphere via an autoencoder; WaveDINO explicitly MODELS and REMOVES the atmospheric component with
  terrain + FM conditioning + GNSS validation. Together they define a two-pronged InSAR-denoising design
  for WS1: (a) correct atmosphere (WaveDINO-style terrain/ramp/wavelet), THEN (b) extract deformation
  anomaly in the residual (Rouet-Leduc AE / Deep-SVDD).
- **Three recurring doctrines reinforced in ONE paper:**
  1. **Wavelet multi-scale separation** (Thread: WinoTS 2609.39337, water-level CWT 2511.20936) — again
     the winning representation for a geophysical signal.
  2. **Physics as structure** (Thread I) — the phase-ramp head hard-codes the known low-order planar
     structure of atmospheric delay; terrain conditioning encodes the elevation-delay physics.
  3. **FROZEN foundation-model features as conditioning** (DINOv3) — a cheap way to inject strong priors
     without training a huge backbone; a concrete data-point for our EO-FM encoder trade (CR-01) that
     one can CONDITION on a frozen FM rather than fine-tune it (Kaggle-budget friendly).
- GNSS cross-validation is exactly the kind of INDEPENDENT ground-truth check our InSAR layer needs —
  and we already plan GNSS (ties DetEQ 2412.00264); use GNSS both to CORRECT and to VALIDATE InSAR.

## Caveats / honesty
- Validated on VOLCANIC deformation (Laguna del Maule, Campi Flegrei) with good GNSS networks and
  ~50×50 km crops — NOT Himalayan landslide terrain; the planar-ramp assumption may break in steep
  multi-scale Himalayan topography (authors note larger scenes need higher-order curvature). Must be
  re-validated on Himalayan InSAR with local GNSS before reliance; 3%/19% misfit-reduction figures are
  volcano-specific.
- Needs a GNSS network for validation — Himalayan GNSS density is lower; our sparse GNSS may limit both
  training-label quality and validation confidence.
- Trained partly on SYNTHETIC deformation — synthetic-to-real gap must be checked for our regime.
