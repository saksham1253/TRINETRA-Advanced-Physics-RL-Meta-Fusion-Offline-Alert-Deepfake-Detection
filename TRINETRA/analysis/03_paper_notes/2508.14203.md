## Problem & why it matters
Comprehensive, recent survey of deep-learning Video Anomaly Detection (VAD) organized by
supervision level (fully/weakly/one-class/unsupervised + online/active/continual) and by
application domain (human-centric / vehicle-centric / ENVIRONMENT-centric). The
environment-centric slice is directly relevant to TRINETRA's CCTV/River-Eye hazard-watch role.
Its explicit CHALLENGE taxonomy is a ready-made failure-mode checklist for our WS4.

## Core content — challenge taxonomy (the reusable part)
- Data/annotation: C1 rarity + class imbalance; C2 limited/difficult labeling; C3 ambiguity in
  DEFINING an anomaly + context specificity (an "anomaly" is deployment-defined — matches our
  worry that a hazard ≠ a generic VAD anomaly).
- Spatiotemporal: C4 long-term temporal dependencies; C5 multi-agent / crowded scenes;
  C6 feature-abstraction level.
- Robustness/generalization: C7 environmental variation + noise (weather, lighting — exactly our
  field condition); C8 DOMAIN SHIFT + cross-scene generalization (the cross-camera leakage theme,
  FM-01); C9 open-set / novelty (new anomaly types unseen in training); C10 CONCEPT DRIFT +
  evolving normality (A14 theme).
- Supervision taxonomy (§III): fully-supervised (rare, needs frame labels), weakly-supervised
  (video-level labels — the practical SOTA, UCF-Crime/XD-Violence), one-class/semi (train on
  normal only — MemAE/HF²-VAD lineage), unsupervised, plus online/active/continual adaptation.

## Data / splits / generalization (as surveyed)
- Standard benchmarks: UCF-Crime, XD-Violence, ShanghaiTech, Avenue, Ped1/2. Survey explicitly
  flags cross-scene generalization (C8) and open-set (C9) as largely UNSOLVED → in-dataset AUC
  overstates deployment readiness.

## Key takeaways (survey-level, no single number)
- The field is fragmented; most reported gains are in-dataset; robustness/generalization/drift are
  the open problems — i.e. the dimensions that matter MOST for a safety deployment are the weakest.
- "Evolving normality" (C10) means a fixed VAD model degrades over time at a fixed camera.
