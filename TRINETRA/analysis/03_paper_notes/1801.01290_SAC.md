## Content (own words)
- Problem: model-free deep RL has high sample complexity + brittle hyperparameter
  sensitivity, limiting real-world use.
- Core idea: off-policy actor-critic in the MAXIMUM-ENTROPY framework — the actor maximizes
  reward AND policy entropy ("succeed while acting as randomly as possible"). Combines
  off-policy replay (sample-efficient) with a stable stochastic actor. Uses a reparameterized
  gradient (Eq.13) extending DDPG-style gradients to any tractable stochastic policy.
  Algorithm 1: alternate soft Q / value updates and policy update sampling from replay D.
- Claims: SOTA on continuous-control benchmarks; notably STABLE across random seeds (vs
  brittle prior off-policy methods). (Exact numbers pruned — not verified.)

## Relevance to us
- Component: WS5. SAC is the maximum-entropy backbone; TD3+BC and the online PPO stage draw
  on this lineage. Takeaway: entropy regularization = built-in exploration/robustness, and
  seed-stability matters a lot for a life-safety policy we must trust.
- For TRINETRA the alarm action space is likely DISCRETE (alert levels) → discrete-SAC or
  the entropy idea transfers, but the continuous-control results don't directly apply.
- Transfer hypothesis: SAC's stability claim is on dense-reward control; our reward is sparse
  + highly imbalanced (miss=-100) — stability UNVALIDATED there.
- confidence: med-high on method (well-known), low on direct transfer.
