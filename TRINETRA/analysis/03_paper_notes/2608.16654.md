## What it is
A clean, reusable recipe for **OOD detection + UQ** on an image classifier (ResNet-34, Galaxy Zoo
DECaLS, 9 morphology classes). Three configs: cross-entropy baseline; **IsoMaxPlus** loss;
**IsoMaxPlus + MC-Dropout**.
- **IsoMaxPlus** replaces SoftMax logits with **distance-based class representations** (isotropy
  maximization) → preserves inter-class separability, enables OOD detection **with NO extra
  architecture, NO hyperparameter tuning, and NO outlier examples during training**.
- MC-Dropout adds a cheap Bayesian approximation (multiple stochastic forward passes).

## Numbers (as-published)
- OOD: IsoMaxPlus raises **TNR@TPR95 by ~90%** vs cross-entropy, keeping 97% accuracy.
- Calibration: **ECE 0.0095 → 0.0026 (≈73% lower)** with IsoMaxPlus; 0.0033 (≈65%) with +MC-Dropout.
- Interpretable reliability signal: misclassified/underconfident samples show **higher predictive
  entropy + lower minimum-distance score** → a usable flag for unreliable predictions.
- OOD test classes held out entirely (Merger, Irr, Bulge-Round, Merger-Dist) — proper open-set eval.

- **Directly addresses our #1 safety need: detecting inputs the model has never seen.** A Himalayan

  → candidate unifying design for the WS8 anomaly/OOD head.

  metric** (Rule 19) — pairs with our conformal/abstention plan.

  constraints.
