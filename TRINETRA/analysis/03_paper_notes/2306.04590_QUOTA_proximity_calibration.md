## Content (own words)
- Finding: "PROXIMITY BIAS" — deep classifiers are MORE OVERCONFIDENT on LOW-PROXIMITY samples
  (data in SPARSE regions of the distribution) than on dense-region samples, so miscalibration is
  INCONSISTENT across the input space. Verified over 504 pretrained ImageNet models. Key points:
  (1) bias exists across architectures/sizes; (2) TRANSFORMERS more susceptible than CNNs;
  (3) it PERSISTS even after temperature scaling; (4) models overfit low-proximity samples more.
- Method: proximity = average distance to K (e.g. 10) nearest neighbors. ProCal = plug-and-play
  recalibration that adjusts confidence based on proximity, with a theoretical guarantee (Brier
  score after their bin-mean-shift is asymptotically bounded by the original). New metric PIECE
  (proximity-informed ECE) to measure residual proximity-dependent miscalibration. Works on
  balanced, long-tail, and distribution-shift settings.

## What it CHANGED in our thinking (required for quota)
- This is a SHARP, directly-applicable warning for TRINETRA's rare-event tail. Our most
  consequential inputs — pre-failure InSAR signatures, GLOF precursors — are precisely
  LOW-PROXIMITY (sparse, few analogues in training data). ProCal's finding says our models will
  be MOST OVERCONFIDENT exactly where we most need honest uncertainty, and standard temperature
  scaling will NOT fix it. ACTION: calibration for TRINETRA cannot be a single global temperature;
  it must be proximity-/density-aware (ProCal-style) AND wrapped by distribution-free coverage
  (EnbPI 2010.09107) — the two are complementary (ProCal fixes point-confidence shape, EnbPI
  gives interval coverage). Add "proximity-aware calibration + PIECE monitoring" to the UQ spec.
- Reinforces the equity theme (shared with 2608.26304): low-proximity = underrepresented
  conditions/zones, so uncalibrated overconfidence systematically harms the rarest, often most
  vulnerable, cases. Another concrete Rule-19 hook: report calibration PER proximity/region, not
  just a global ECE.
- Also disciplines encoder choice (CR-01): transformers are MORE prone to proximity bias than
  CNNs — a cost to weigh against the fancy EO-FM transformer encoders (DINOv2/CROMA/Copernicus-FM/
  AnySat), adding to the CR-02 "simplicity" pressure.
