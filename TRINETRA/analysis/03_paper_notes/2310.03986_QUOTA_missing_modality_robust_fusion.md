## Content (own words)
- Finding: multimodal networks are NOT inherently robust — performance drops SIGNIFICANTLY when
  one/more modalities are absent at test time. Modalities go missing due to hardware
  limitations/FAILURES, privacy, or acquisition constraints — exactly our ground reality.
- Method: a SIMPLE, PARAMETER-EFFICIENT adaptation of a PRETRAINED multimodal network: modulate
  intermediate features to compensate for the missing modality, using <1% of total parameters.
  Applies across many modality combinations and tasks WITHOUT training a separate dedicated
  network per combination. Can even outperform dedicated per-combination networks in some cases.
- Evidence: 5 multimodal tasks across 7 datasets (segmentation, sentiment, classification, etc.);
  the adaptation PARTIALLY bridges the missing-modality performance gap and beats prior robust-MML
  methods.

## What it CHANGED in our thinking (required for quota)
- Directly addresses CR-04 / FM-15 (missing & unreliable inputs) at the FUSION layer. TRINETRA
  WILL routinely lose modalities: Sentinel-1 revisit gaps, cloud over Sentinel-2, LoRa node
  outages (2307.04016 LTE/solar failures), CCTV offline. This paper confirms naive fusion
  degrades badly AND offers a cheap, concrete design pattern: train/pretrain the fused model, then
  add a tiny (<1% params) adaptation that modulates features for whichever modalities are present
  — instead of maintaining 2^M separate models for every subset of M sensors (combinatorially
  hopeless for us). ACTION: adopt "one fused backbone + lightweight missing-modality adaptation"
  as the baseline fusion-robustness design for GMF, rather than per-combination experts; adds a
  concrete comparator to CR-05 (gating) and reduces model/config debt (ties 2103.09783).
- The <1% parameter cost matters for our Kaggle/edge budget: robustness without a model zoo.
- Honest limitation it names (and we must inherit): adaptation only PARTIALLY bridges the gap —
  missing a modality still costs accuracy. So the SAFETY response to a lost critical modality
  must be to widen uncertainty and bias toward caution (shield), NOT to pretend the fused output
  is as reliable as full-modality (Rule 19). Ties EnbPI/ProCal: missing modality should INFLATE
  predicted uncertainty, and the conformal/calibration layer must reflect that.
