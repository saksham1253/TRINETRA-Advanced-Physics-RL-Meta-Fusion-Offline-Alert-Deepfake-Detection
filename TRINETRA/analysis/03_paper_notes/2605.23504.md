## What it is
A self-supervised **multivariate time-series anomaly detector** that makes "normal" an **explicit,
compact, directionally-coherent geometric region** in embedding space — then flags geometric
deviations. Key design:
- Problem with contrastive SSL: it shapes the latent space only INDIRECTLY via pair-sampling
  heuristics → no explicit control over the geometry that distance-based scoring needs (how tightly
  normal is grouped; whether distances are directionally meaningful).
- **VACE (Velocity-Aligned Channel Embeddings):** a channel-aware encoder trained with a
  **velocity-consistency objective** — **no negatives, no synthetic anomalies** — so normal
  trajectories are locally smooth and aligned.
- **Scoring = multiplicative combination** of a **Mahalanobis positional score** (off-distribution)
  × a **velocity-bank directional score** (dynamically atypical) → flags points that are BOTH off
  the normal manifold AND moving atypically.

## Results & eval discipline (as published)
- "**Despite its simplicity, VACE achieves SOTA on TSB-AD-M under rigorous evaluation, significantly
  outperforming more complex methods trained on substantially larger budgets.**" → CR-02.
- **Evaluation metric guidance (important, portable):** thresholding is orthogonal to scoring quality
  and can MASK ranking differences → they give PRIMARY emphasis to **threshold-free** metrics:
  AUC-ROC, AUC-PR, and **VUS-ROC / VUS-PR** (Volume Under the Surface), plus Point-F1 and **Range-F1**
  (segment-weighted recall). Train set anomaly-free; labels only in test (realistic one-class setup).

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (WS8 TSAD + the "normal=geometry" cluster + eval)
- **Fifth and most explicit member of the "normal as geometry" cluster**: prototype-head (2610.06241),
  normal-subspace NCL (2610.06453), IsoMaxPlus distance-logits (2608.16654), federated global prototype
  (2608.06876), and now VACE's **Mahalanobis + velocity-bank** manifold. → WS8 doctrine crystallizes:
  **learn a tight normal manifold with an explicit distance metric; combine a POSITIONAL (where) and a
  DIRECTIONAL/DYNAMIC (how it's moving) score.** The directional/velocity score is especially apt for
  landslide creep → acceleration (Fukuzono 1/v) and river-stage rise-rate — anomaly isn't just "far
  from normal" but "moving wrong," exactly VACE's multiplicative insight.
- **"No negatives, no synthetic anomalies"** fits our constraint: we have abundant normal Himalayan
  streams and almost no labeled failures. VACE trains only on anomaly-free data.
- **Eval-metric rule for our TSAD harness:** emphasize threshold-free VUS-ROC/VUS-PR + Range-F1 (segment
  recall) over Point-F1; thresholding is a separate downstream decision (ties to our cost-loss Value
  Score as the final operating-point metric). Add to the WS8/eval-harness spec.
- CR-02 corroborator #: simple velocity-geometry beats larger-budget deep methods under rigorous eval.
