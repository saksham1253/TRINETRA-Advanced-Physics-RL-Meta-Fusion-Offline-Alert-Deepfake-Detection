## What it is
First SSL pipeline that pretrains on **SAR-INTENSITY imagery ALONE** (no optical grounding) for downstream
EO tasks. Key design:
- **Temporal pretext tasks on SAR time series:**
  - **Time-Difference prediction (TD)** — predict the change between two SAR acquisitions.
  - **Future-Frame prediction (FF)** — predict the next SAR frame.
- **Spatial objective via masking** added to both TD and FF (masked prediction → forces spatial structure
  learning, MAE-style, on top of the temporal task).
- **Joint training:** multi-task (MTL) + **curriculum learning** — pretrain on one pretext task, then use
  it as init to jointly train both (eqs 9–10: L_stage1 = L_Task1; L_stage2 = λL_Task1 + (1−λ)L_Task2).
  Curriculum retains the strengths of individual tasks (cites prior sequential-pretext work).
- Evaluated on **SICKLE** benchmark (crop-type mapping).

## Verified numbers (abstract + §4 head)
- **84.9% IoU on crop-type mapping** (SICKLE).
- **+15.3 pt over OPTICAL baselines** — SAR beats optical for this all-weather task.
- **+2.2 pt over existing SAR baselines.**

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (WS1 encoder + CR-01)
1. **Directly advances the CR-01 EO-FM encoder trade study.** The abstract is a MAP of the SAR
   foundation-model landscape we must evaluate:
   - **Multimodal RS-FMs grounding SAR in optical:** TerraMind, CopernicusFM (= our Copernicus-FM
     2503.11849 candidate) — SAR representations learned via contrastive alignment to optical.
   - **SAR-SPECIFIC FMs:** SAR-JEPA, SARMAE, SAR-W-MixMAE — focused on target detection, flood mapping,
     land-cover. (Flood-mapping relevance for WS7.)
   - This paper = first SAR-intensity-ONLY temporal-pretext SSL. → add all of these to the P7 encoder
     trade matrix; SAR-JEPA/SARMAE/SAR-W-MixMAE especially (flood-mapping pretraining = closer to our
     task than agri).
2. **Temporal pretext tasks (TD/FF) are a near-perfect template for our InSAR/SAR anomaly encoder.** Our
   SSL masked-InSAR encoder + forecast-residual anomaly stage is essentially FF (future-frame) + masking.
   This is independent evidence that *temporal* pretext (predict change / predict next frame) + *masking*
   is the right SSL objective for a SAR deformation encoder — not just static masked autoencoding.
   Corroborates WaveDINO (2606.16795) and the SSL4EO knee findings; sharpens our encoder design.
3. **Curriculum over naive MTL** (eqs 9–10): pretrain one pretext task, then jointly fine-tune — a cheap,
   Kaggle-budget-friendly recipe to combine our masked-reconstruction + forecast-residual objectives
   without destabilizing training. Concrete, reusable.
4. **Honest SAR-vs-optical head-to-head: SAR +15.3 pt over optical** on an all-weather task. Strong
   quantitative support for our SAR-first (cloud-robust) stance in the monsoon Himalaya where optical is
   frequently cloud-blocked — same lesson as 2512.24117 (SAR>optical for GLOF).

## Caveats / honesty
- **Domain = AGRICULTURE (crop-type mapping), not geohazard deformation.** What transfers is the SSL
  RECIPE (temporal pretext + masking + curriculum on SAR intensity), NOT the crop task. Re-measure on
  deformation/InSAR (Rule 16). Crucially, this uses SAR INTENSITY, whereas our InSAR deformation signal
  is in the PHASE/coherence domain — the pretext tasks may need re-casting for interferometric phase.
- 84.9% IoU is SICKLE-specific; cite as evidence the recipe WORKS, not as our expected performance.
- FT-PARTIAL: full experiment tables (ablations of TD vs FF vs MTL vs curriculum) partially pruned;
  headline IoU + the curriculum formulation verified, per-ablation deltas NOT fully verified (D-008).
- SAR-intensity-only avoids optical dependence — good for our cloud problem — but gives up the
  optical-SAR complementarity that TerraMind/CopernicusFM exploit; the trade must be measured, not assumed.
