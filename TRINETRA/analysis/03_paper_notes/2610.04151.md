## What it is
A large open-licence benchmark of the two **C2PA "soft binding"** families used to recover a stripped
provenance manifest: **invisible watermarks** (read from the content) and **fingerprints** (looked up
in a registry). One protocol, licence-audited open models, public media, FMR calibrated on held-out
negatives, source-level bootstrap CIs.
- Watermarking: 25 image / 7 audio / 7 video configs from 12 methods (quality, robustness, FP, cost).
- Fingerprinting: 35 methods, registries up to 98,985 images, partial edits + adversarial attacks.

## Key findings (as-published, abstract)
- **Best balance:** PixelSeal (image+video watermark), AudioSeal (audio).
- **Caution — false positives:** every TrustMark variant's error-correcting detector **fired on
  5.9–15.3% of UNMARKED images** → "a verifier should test the expected payload" (don't trust a bare
  watermark hit).
- Fingerprints: copy detectors (non-commercial) up to **75.5%** of transformed images at pair-level
  FMR 1e−7; DINOv2 (best permissive) 65.0%. Near-copies in catalogues dominate false matches.
- **Families fail differently** on attacked copies: union of watermark+fingerprint covered 69% of
  image copies; fingerprints better for audio, expected-key watermark better for video.
- Platform fragility: embedding a watermark moved the ISCC code of 62.0% of images past match
  threshold; **colour conversion changed watermark bits between x86 and ARM hosts**.

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (WS2 provenance + WS3 trust)
- For the crowd/CCTV corroboration layer, **content provenance (C2PA) is the complement to deepfake
  detection**: if an incoming "hazard" image/video carries a verifiable manifest, trust rises; if
  not, it stays low-weight (consistent with 2609.07455/2511.21364 low-weight-crowd doctrine).
- **Hard caution for us:** a watermark/fingerprint HIT is NOT proof — 5.9–15.3% false fires on
  unmarked content, and the signal is fragile across platforms (x86 vs ARM bit changes matter for
  our edge/ARM nodes). So provenance can RAISE confidence but must never be a sole gate (Rule 19).
- Reinforces the WS3 "multi-source corroboration" principle from 2604.24404: combine provenance +
  cross-channel agreement, never a single soft binding.
