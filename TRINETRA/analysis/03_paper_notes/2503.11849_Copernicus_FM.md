## Content (own words)
- Contribution (3 parts): (1) Copernicus-Pretrain — massive pretraining dataset, 18.7M aligned
  images spanning ALL major Copernicus Sentinel missions, surface → atmosphere; (2)
  Copernicus-FM — a unified foundation model that processes ANY spectral OR non-spectral sensor
  modality via extended dynamic HYPERNETWORKS + flexible METADATA encoding; (3) Copernicus-Bench
  — systematic benchmark, 15 hierarchical downstream tasks (preprocessing → specialized apps)
  per Sentinel mission.
- Motivation: prior EO FMs limited in (a) sensor diversity (mostly S1/S2 surface only, excluding
  temporally-rich S3/S5P atmosphere), (b) model flexibility (fixed spectral sensors), (c)
  evaluation breadth. Connects EO + weather + climate.
- Verified (Table 3 licenses — matters for us): Cloud-S2 CC0-1.0; Cloud-S3 CC-BY-4.0;
  EuroSAT-S1/S2 CC-BY-4.0 / MIT; BigEarthNet-S1/S2 CDLA-Permissive-1.0. Code/data/models at
  github.com/zhu-xlab/Copernicus-FM.
