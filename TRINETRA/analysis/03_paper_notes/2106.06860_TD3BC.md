## Content (own words)
- Problem: offline RL methods add lots of complexity (new hyperparameters, generative models,
  secondary components) to fight extrapolation error on OOD actions — hurting
  reproducibility, tuning, and runtime.
- Core idea (re-implementable, very simple): take an ONLINE algorithm (TD3) and make two
  minimal changes: (1) add a BEHAVIOR-CLONING term to the policy update (keep actions close
  to the dataset), weighted by lambda normalized by the mean |Q|; (2) NORMALIZE the state
  features. That's it — no generative model, no extra critic beyond TD3's twin critics.
- Evidence seen (D4RL table, Tier-1 numbers I actually read): TD3+BC vs CQL on normalized
  scores, e.g. Hopper-Medium: TD3+BC 99.5±1.0 vs CQL 72.0 vs AWAC 30.0; Walker2d-Medium:
  TD3+BC 79.7±1.8 vs CQL 30.1; HalfCheetah-Medium: TD3+BC 42.8±0.3 vs CQL 37.4. TD3+BC
  matches or beats CQL on most of these while "more than halving" runtime.
- Claim: matches SOTA offline RL with minimal changes + ~2x faster.

## Relevance to us
- Component: WS5. TD3+BC is THE minimalist baseline for CR-03 (multi-stage vs simple offline
  RL). Takeaway: strong evidence that a simple BC-regularized method rivals CQL — supports
  the parsimony principle (Q0.3) and argues we should try TD3+BC BEFORE committing to CQL/IQL
  for the alarm policy.
- Note on evidence: these D4RL numbers are locomotion, continuous-action. Our alarm MDP is
  discrete-action, sparse, tiny — direct transfer is a HYPOTHESIS, but the "simple first"
  lesson transfers regardless.
- confidence: high on the method + the specific numbers I read; low on direct domain transfer.
- CONTRADICTION: strengthens CR-03's "one-step/simple side".
