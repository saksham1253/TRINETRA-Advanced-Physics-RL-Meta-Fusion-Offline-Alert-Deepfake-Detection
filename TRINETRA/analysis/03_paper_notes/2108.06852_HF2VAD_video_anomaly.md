## Content (own words)
- Problem: Video Anomaly Detection (VAD) — abnormal events are RARE and UNBOUNDED, so you
  cannot collect them in advance; standard solution = train UNSUPERVISED on NORMAL data, flag
  outliers at test.
- Core idea (re-implementable): HF²-VAD fuses two complementary unsupervised signals:
  (1) ML-MemAE-SC — a multi-level MEMORY-augmented autoencoder with skip connections that
      memorizes normal OPTICAL-FLOW patterns → abnormal events give large flow-reconstruction
      error; (2) a Conditional VAE (CVAE) that predicts the NEXT FRAME conditioned on the
      reconstructed flow. Because frame prediction is conditioned on reconstructed flow, a
      poorly reconstructed (abnormal) flow further degrades the predicted frame → anomalies
      become MORE detectable. Final anomaly score (Eq.9) = weighted z-normalized sum of
      reconstruction + prediction errors: S = w_r·(S_r−μ_r)/σ_r + w_p·(S_p−μ_p)/σ_p.
- Evidence: Fig.1 shows the hybrid beats flow-recon-only and prediction-only in AUROC on
  CUHK Avenue; MNIST toy study ablates memory-AE variants. (Full tables pruned — not verified.)

## Relevance to us
- Component: WS4 (CCTV / River-Eye video anomaly). STRONG: TRINETRA's camera layer must flag
  abnormal scene dynamics (debris flow, surging river, slope movement) with NO labeled
  disaster footage → this unsupervised-on-normal paradigm is exactly right. Two takeaways:
  (1) memory-augmented AE prevents the "AE reconstructs anomalies too well" failure (a known
  VAD pitfall — relevant to FM catalogue); (2) the z-normalized score fusion (Eq.9) is a
  clean, reusable template for combining multiple anomaly signals (feeds GMF + anomaly-score
  calibration, and the WS8 fusion head).
- Transfer hypothesis: CUHK-Avenue pedestrian scenes ≠ mountain river/slope CCTV; the method
  transfers but normal-pattern memory must be learned per-camera (domain/placement specific) —
  ties the per-camera calibration + drift concern (R-14).
- confidence: high on method + relevance; med on transfer (scene-domain gap). Code public
  (github.com/LiUzHiAn/hf2vad). Feeds WS4 design (P7) + FM (AE-generalizes-to-anomaly).
