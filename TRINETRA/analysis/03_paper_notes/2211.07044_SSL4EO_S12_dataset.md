## Content (own words)
- Contribution: SSL4EO-S12 — unlabeled, GLOBAL, multimodal (Sentinel-2 L1C + S2 L2A + Sentinel-1
  GRD), multi-SEASONAL (4 snapshots/location) corpus. 250K globally-distributed locations,
  3M patches of 2640×2640 m, deliberately NON-overlapping for geospatial coverage. Positioned
  as the largest/most-generic multispectral+SAR SSL dataset.
- Benchmarks 4 SSL methods — MoCo-v2, DINO, MAE, data2vec — on scene classification, semantic
  segmentation, change detection; shows SSL4EO-S12 pretraining beats prior datasets.
- Verified KEY finding (Table XI, BigEarthNet-10% linear probe vs S2-L1C pretrain size):
  100K→64%, 250K→73%, 500K→78%, 750K→81%, 1M→82%. ⇒ sharp gains to ~750K then SATURATION
  (diminishing returns beyond 750K), depends on model size.
