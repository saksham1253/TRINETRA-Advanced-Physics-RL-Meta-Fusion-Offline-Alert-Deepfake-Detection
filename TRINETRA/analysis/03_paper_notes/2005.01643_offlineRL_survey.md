## Content (own words)
- Scope: the canonical tutorial on offline (batch) RL — concepts, why naive off-policy fails
  offline, families of solutions, open problems.
- Core framing (re-usable): offline RL turns static datasets into decision policies without
  online interaction; the central obstacle is distributional shift (the learned policy visits
  (s,a) the behavior policy did not, so bootstrapped values are unreliable). Bellman operator
  B^π is an ℓ∞ contraction (basis for value iteration). Two algorithm families derived:
  Q-learning (implicit argmax policy) and actor-critic.
- Taxonomy (from abstract/intro + prior knowledge, flagged): policy-constraint methods,
  value-regularization (→ CQL), importance-sampling, model-based offline RL, one-step methods.
  Open problems: reliable off-policy EVALUATION (OPE), distribution shift, hyperparameter
  selection without online tuning.

## Relevance to us
- Component: WS5 orientation. Takeaway: this survey is the map for the whole offline-RL
  design decision (CR-03). The "no online tuning / OPE is hard" open problem is CRITICAL for
  TRINETRA — we cannot online-tune an alarm policy on real disasters, so OPE quality gates
  the entire RL approach (ties FM-08).
- Directly supports the P15 plan to benchmark one-step vs iterative offline RL and to treat
  OPE honestly rather than assume it works.
- confidence: high as an orienting map; specific method numbers deferred to the primary
  papers (CQL/IQL already noted).
