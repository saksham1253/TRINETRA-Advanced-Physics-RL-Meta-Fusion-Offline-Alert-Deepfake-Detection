## Problem & why it matters
Surveillance data are DISTRIBUTED (many cameras/sites), WEAKLY labeled, and resource-constrained —
exactly the TRINETRA River-Eye / CCTV situation (privacy + bandwidth forbid centralizing raw video).
This paper does FEDERATED WSVAD: train only a compact MIL scorer across clients; a frozen VLM
verifies only high-scoring suspect segments post hoc. Directly informs our privacy-preserving,
edge-distributed video design AND gives a cautionary result on VLM fragility.

## Core idea / method (re-implementable sketch)
- Federated MIL anomaly scorer: only a small MIL scorer is trained across clients (raw video stays
  local → privacy + bandwidth). 
- SELECTIVE VLM verification: a FROZEN VLM re-checks only the high-scoring suspect segments (cheap,
  post-hoc) — a two-stage cascade (cheap scorer → expensive verifier only on suspects).
- Two VLM feedback interfaces: (a) TEXT-GENERATION (parse Yes/No text) and (b) LOGIT-BASED (continuous
  anomaly score from next-token Yes/No probabilities — parser-free).

## Data / splits
- UCF-Crime; VLMs InternVL3.5-2B and Qwen3-VL-2B-Instruct. Metrics: frame-AUC, frame-AP.

## Key results (as stated)
- TEXT-generation verification CAN improve frame-AUC but is HIGHLY SENSITIVE to prompt, parser, model
  choice, and temporal smoothing — Table III shows swings from −1.364 to +1.553 AUC across prompt
  variants (R5…R13) and massive dependence on post-processing (+PP). I.e. fragile / unreliable.
- LOGIT-based parser-free interface gives a FIXED signal that improves BOTH frame-AUC and frame-AP
  over the MIL baseline across both VLMs WITHOUT temporal post-processing in its main config → robust.
- Takeaway: a next-token logit score is a simpler, more stable VLM-verification signal than parsing
  generated text.

## Assumptions & limitations
- UCF-Crime only; 2-billion-param VLMs still heavy for a true edge node (verification likely at a
  gateway/cloud tier, not the ₹2000 sensor). Preprint, 6pp.
- The text-gen fragility is itself the load-bearing negative result (prompt-sensitivity = reliability risk).
