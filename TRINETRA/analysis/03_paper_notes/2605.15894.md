## Problem & why it matters
Classify wildfire SMOKE DENSITY (Light / Moderate / Heavy) from satellite patches WITH calibrated
uncertainty, so low-confidence predictions can be deferred. Directly fuses two P2 themes TRINETRA
needs: (a) a compact edge-feasible vision backbone for smoke, (b) evidential UQ + selective
prediction as a false-alarm control. Ties E-07 (act only when confident) and 2306.04590 (proximity
calibration) and the selective-prediction lever.

## Core idea / method (re-implementable sketch)
- Backbone: EfficientNet-B3 + CBAM (convolutional block attention: channel + spatial attention).
- Head: EVIDENTIAL deep-learning classifier — outputs Dirichlet concentration params (evidence)
  instead of softmax; yields decomposable uncertainty: VACUITY (lack of evidence → epistemic/OOD
  signal) and DISSONANCE (conflicting evidence).
- Training objective: evidential loss (expected cross-entropy under Dirichlet + KL regularizer to
  penalize misleading evidence).
- Selective prediction: rank by vacuity, keep most-certain fraction.

## Data / splits
- "Wildfire Detection" dataset, 16,298 satellite patches, 3 severity classes.
- Metrics: weighted/unweighted accuracy, ECE, correlation of uncertainty vs error, selective-pred curve.

## Key results (as stated)
- 93.8% weighted test accuracy (91.1% unweighted); ECE = 0.0274 (well-calibrated).
- Selective prediction: retaining the 50% most-certain patches → 96.7% accuracy (Table 4 trend:
  accuracy rises monotonically as low-vacuity fraction retained; "near-perfect on high-confidence").
- Uncertainty decomposition: vacuity ρ=0.262 vs error, dissonance ρ=0.277 vs error; the "Moderate"
  (transitional smoke) class correctly shows the HIGHEST vacuity (mean 0.187) — model flags its own
  ambiguous boundary cases.
- Image-quality degradation → uncertainty rises monotonically → vacuity is a practical scan-quality measure.

## Assumptions & limitations
- Stated: single dataset; 3-class severity is a simplification. Preprint, single author.
- Inferred: no cross-region / cross-sensor test → in-dataset calibration may not hold under shift
  (the recurring FM-01 / 2606.07593 caveat). ECE measured on-distribution only.
