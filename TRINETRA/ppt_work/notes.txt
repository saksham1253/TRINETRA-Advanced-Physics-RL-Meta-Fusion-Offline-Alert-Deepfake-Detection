########## SLIDE 2 NOTES ##########
Problem: mountain India faces landslides, flash floods, cloudbursts and glacial-lake floods, and each is watched by a separate system.
Three gaps we attack first. Gap 1, offline: storms cut towers and power, so app and SMS alerts fail when needed most [12]. Gap 2, IoT: slopes have almost no ground sensors; our IoT prototype node, capped under 2,000 rupees, lets a village watch its own slope. Gap 3, zero-shot: normal AI only knows disasters it was trained on; zero-shot AI matches a scene to a plain sentence, so it can flag a disaster it never saw.
Supporting gaps: silos, late detection, fake media [10], models that look good only on familiar places [7], models that do not know when they are wrong [9][11].
Conclusion from 500+ papers: not done yet. Parts exist, but no single system combines offline alerts, IoT ground truth, zero-shot AI and physics checks.

########## SLIDE 3 NOTES ##########
Solution: three eyes, one physics referee, one decision. Fuse, verify, a human decides, warn by tier.
If it is unsure, it abstains and escalates. It never guesses silently.
Five things we build: the Himalayan datacube, zero-shot plus anomaly AI, the physics and fusion brain, the offline alert stack, and our IoT prototype node.
Zero-shot in simple words: like recognising a zebra from 'a horse with stripes'. A new hazard needs only a new sentence, not retraining. That is why the framework can extend to any hazard with a visible or measurable sign. Earthquakes are the exception: alerts after onset only.
The matrix summarises our synthesis of 500+ papers: each existing family covers some parts; none covers all.
An IP address does not locate a person, so we warn by place. We complement IMD, CWC and NDMA; we never replace them.

########## SLIDE 4 NOTES ##########
Workflow: sense, detect, verify, physics gate. Do the eyes agree? If not, re-check and test for sensor faults.
Then fuse into one risk score with confidence. Is it above the threshold? Does a human approve? Only then alert by tier.
The IoT edge shortcut: if a node sees a sharp tilt plus heavy rain, it can sound the local siren in seconds, even with no network.
Below: DFD level 0 and level 1, and the offline-first alert path: T0 IoT node, T1 siren, T2 signed LoRa mesh, T3 CAP to SACHET.
The safety shield may only raise an alert, never lower it [5].
