## 1. Ek line mein summary (slide ke liye)

India mein ab **lagbhag har din kahin na kahin extreme weather** hota hai: 2024 mein Jan–Sep ke 274 mein se 255 din.
Sirf us period mein **3,238 logon ki maut** hui aur **~2.36 lakh ghar** damage hue (CSE / Down To Earth, 2024).
Jahan early warning acchi hai, wahan maut bahut kam hoti hai: Odisha 1999 super cyclone mein ~10,000 maut,
jabki Fani 2019 mein 64. Lekin India mein landslide, GLOF aur forest fire ki warning abhi bhi kamzor hai,
aur aapda ke time **fake news** relief ko aur mushkil bana deti hai.

## 7. Early warning (aur offline delivery) kitni kaam ki hai

| Evidence | Number | Source | Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jin deshon mein early warning coverage kam hai, wahan disaster mortality | **~8 guna zyada** (comprehensive coverage wale deshon ke comparison mein) | WMO + UNDRR, *Global Status of MHEWS* 2023 | A |
| Storm / heatwave ki sirf 24 ghante pehle warning | damage **~30% kam** | Global Commission on Adaptation, *Adapt Now* (2019) | A |
| Developing countries mein EWS pe US$800 million kharch | har saal **US$3–16 billion** ka loss bach sakta hai | Global Commission on Adaptation (2019) | A |
| Odisha cyclone deaths | 1999 super cyclone ~10,000 → Phailin 2013 ~44 → Fani 2019 **64** (~12 lakh logon ka evacuation) | OSDMA / IMD / UN | A |
| Cyclone Biparjoy, Gujarat 2023 | ~1 lakh log pehle hi evacuate kiye; cyclone se seedhi maut lagbhag zero bataayi gayi | Gujarat govt / NDMA | B |
| Ahmedabad Heat Action Plan | ~**1,190 maut har saal** bachne ka estimate (2014–15) | Hess et al., *J. Environ. Public Health* 2018 | A |
| India ka national alert system | NDMA **SACHET**: CAP-based, SMS + Cell Broadcast trials (2023 se) | NDMA / DoT | A |

**Offline kyun zaroori hai (qualitative, Conf. A):** Kerala 2018, Chamoli 2021, Sikkim 2023 aur Wayanad 2024
jaisi aapdaon mein bijli aur mobile towers toot gaye, aur log ghanto/dino tak cut-off rahe. Wahan sirf
internet-app wali warning kaam nahi karti. Isliye TRINETRA ki delivery mein **SMS / Cell Broadcast / LoRa mesh /
sirens / community radio** fallback chain hai (dekho `08_ws3_offline_alerting.md`).

## 9. Verification checklist (slide se pehle)

1. **B** wale har number ka original PDF / page kholo aur exact figure + saal note karo.
2. NCRB ADSI aur Crime in India ki tables: `ncrb.gov.in` → Publications.
3. CSE reports: `downtoearth.org.in` → *State of Extreme Weather* (2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 editions).
4. Parliament answers (MHA disaster deaths, CWC flood damage): `sansad.in` → Questions search mein "loss of lives due to floods" ya "hydro-meteorological disasters" search karo.
5. WMO / UNDRR: *Global Status of Multi-Hazard Early Warning Systems 2023*; UNDRR–CRED 1998–2017 report.
6. Slide pe hamesha source ka chhota naam likho, jaise "Source: CSE 2024" ya "NCRB ADSI 2022".
