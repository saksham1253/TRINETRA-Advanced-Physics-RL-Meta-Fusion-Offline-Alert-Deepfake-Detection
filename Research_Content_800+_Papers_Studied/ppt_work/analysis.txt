      || Impact Metric | Details
      || Social Impact | ▸ Lead time: earlier, uncertainty-aware warnings; targets from published benchmarks.

▸ Anti-rumour: deepfake and misinformation checks stop fake photos from driving panic or false calm.
▸ Inclusive: plain multilingual alerts by place, not by phone number or IP; tested with communities.
▸ Human in charge: an operator approves every public alert; the AI only advises.
      || Economic Impact | ▸ Low-cost node: our IoT prototype node, capped under INR 2,000 (BOM pending).
▸ Free data backbone: Sentinel-1/2, GPM IMERG, SMAP, ERA5-Land (licences to be verified).
▸ Reuse CCTV: zero-shot classification on existing road and bridge cameras; no new camera network.
▸ One framework: many hazards instead of a separate system per hazard; less duplicate monitoring.
▸ Protects lifelines: earlier hill-road and bridge closure decisions (benefit to be modelled in pilot).
▸ Open + modular: local teams and startups can extend the stack; costs measured in the pilot.
      || Environmental Impact | ▸ Low power: solar-assisted, duty-cycled nodes; on-device TinyML sends alerts, not raw data.
▸ Orbit-first: slopes, water and vegetation watched from space; no heavy new infrastructure.
▸ Glacial-lake watch: Sentinel-2 and SAR track GLOF risk (designed; validation pending).
▸ Climate adaptation: long-term hazard logs (D1-D4) support planning for changing monsoons.
▸ Small footprint: few nodes per slope, low-power radio; battery life measured in the field.
▸ Staged pilot: shadow mode first, running beside existing systems; no disruption.
      || Target Audience | ▸ Disaster agencies: DEOCs, SDMA, SDRF / NDRF get decision support (complement, not replace).

▸ Communities: village volunteers and panchayats receiving siren and mesh alerts.
▸ Researchers: open benchmark, honest spatial tests; regulation under expert review.
