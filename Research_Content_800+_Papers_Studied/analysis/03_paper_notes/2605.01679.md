## What it is
A **formally-guaranteed differential-privacy** training method for a **life-safety sensor task**
(elderly fall detection from wearable/activity sensors — highly sensitive data). Core idea:
conventional DP adds UNIFORM gradient noise to all samples, which hurts the RARE but critical class.
- **CA-ADP (Class-Aware Adaptive DP):** scales the noise σ per mini-batch by its class composition.
  When a batch is fall-dominated, σ_b = σ_base·(1−α) (LESS noise → **preserve the minority/critical-
  class gradient signal**); when ADL(normal)-dominated, σ_b = σ_base (full noise). Noise range
  [σ_base(1−α), σ_base].
- Standard DP-SGD plumbing (per-sample gradient clipping + Gaussian noise) with a formal **(ε,δ)-DP**
  guarantee + explicit privacy–utility trade-off analysis.
- Results (as published): F-score improvements over conventional DP of **3.3% / 8.5% / 7.5%** on
  SisFall / UP-Fall / MobiAct; Wilcoxon signed-rank confirms consistent gains. 3D-CNN–BiLSTM backbone.

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (privacy for sensitive sensing + rare-event handling + Rule 19)
- **Privacy template for TRINETRA's human-adjacent sensing** (CCTV/River-Eye near homes, activity
  near settlements). CA-ADP shows you can train with a FORMAL (ε,δ) privacy guarantee AND limit the
  accuracy cost — important for consent/ethics/law (flagged for expert review), complementing the
  FEDERATED privacy thread (FedVAR 2608.06876, federated TinyML 2609.27202, federated VLM-verify
  2609.07455). DP and FL are composable: FL keeps raw data local; DP bounds what the shared model
  leaks.
- **The class-imbalance insight is the real transferable gem.** TRINETRA's events (landslide, flash
  flood, GLOF) are EXTREMELY rare vs normal — the same "critical minority class" structure as
  falls-vs-ADL. CA-ADP's lesson: **don't drown the rare-hazard signal in uniform privacy noise (or,
  by analogy, in uniform regularization/augmentation) — protect the minority-class gradient.** This
  generalizes beyond DP to our whole rare-event learning doctrine (class-balanced sampling, focal/cost
  loss, importance-sampling rare rain per IRENE) and gives a privacy-compatible way to do it.
- Adds a CONCRETE privacy mechanism to the deployment/ethics notes: report the (ε,δ) budget and the
  privacy–utility curve as a deliverable, not an afterthought.
