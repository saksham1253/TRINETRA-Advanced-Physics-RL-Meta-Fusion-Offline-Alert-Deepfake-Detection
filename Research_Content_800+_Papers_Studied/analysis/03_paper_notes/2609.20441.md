## Problem & why it matters
Geospatial foundation models (GFMs) give strong flood segmentation but are too big for edge hardware.
This paper distills a 300M-param Prithvi-EO-2.0 teacher into a 0.7M-param EfficientViT-B0 student,
deployable on a Jetson. It hits THREE TRINETRA-critical themes at once: (1) EO foundation model →
compact edge model (CR-01 encoder trade + WS4 edge), (2) pseudo-label amplification of a tiny manual
budget, (3) a BLUNT HONESTY finding — a fixed spectral RULE (MNDWI) is competitive with both deep
models on clean external benchmarks (fresh CR-02 instance in the flood domain).

## Core idea / method (re-implementable sketch)
- Teacher: Prithvi-EO-2.0 (300M), 6 HLS bands (B2,B3,B4,B8A,B11,B12), fine-tuned on 252 labeled
  Sen1Floods11 scenes.
- Student: EfficientViT-B0 (0.7M). Teacher pseudo-labels additional UNLABELED Sentinel-2 imagery →
  grows student training set WITHOUT new annotations (knowledge distillation + pseudo-labeling).
- Compression: activation replacement + quantization-aware training → INT8 TensorRT engine.
- Evaluation grouping hierarchy: chip → flood map → event (prevents chip-level leakage across the
  same event — a correct spatial-CV practice, aligns FM-01).

## Data / splits / generalization
- Train: Sen1Floods11 (252 labeled scenes) + up to 2,500 pseudo-labeled scenes.
- External generalization tests: STURM-Flood (2,675 chips / 46 maps / 29 EMS activations),
  WorldFloods-v2. Explicit acquisition-shift diagnostics (§IV-F).

## Key results (as stated)
- Float student: 0.787 water IoU on Sen1Floods11 test vs 0.822 teacher (gap ~0.035); matches teacher
  on STURM-Flood; below teacher on WorldFloods-v2.
- Pseudo-label scaling 252→2,500 scenes narrows the student-teacher gap (budget amplification works).
- Deployment: 1.5 MB INT8 engine, Jetson Xavier NX, 5.57 ms GPU compute per 512×512 image, ~14 MB
  runtime memory. → a genuine edge profile.
- KEY HONESTY RESULT: a FIXED MNDWI spectral-index threshold is competitive with BOTH deep models on
  the two clean external benchmarks → authors interpret those benchmarks as generalization tests, NOT
  as evidence that learned models beat a spectral rule. (CR-02 again.)

## Assumptions & limitations
- Stated: optical only; cloud cover during active precipitation is a real limitation (near-real-time
  left as future work) — exactly our monsoon-cloud problem; SAR (Sentinel-1) would be needed for
  all-weather. Preprint.
- Inferred: MNDWI competitiveness means the deep model's value is in HARD/shifted scenes, not clean ones.

## Reproducibility
