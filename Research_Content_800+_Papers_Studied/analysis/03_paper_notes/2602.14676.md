## What it is
Defines the **Bus Evacuation Orienteering Problem (BEOP)** — NP-hard: evacuate as many people as
possible by bus within a fixed time T (bus-based to avoid car-evacuation congestion). Solves it with
a **graph-learning deep-RL** method (edge-attention GNN) that, once trained, produces routes in
fractions of a second; an **MILP formulation bounds the optimality gap**. Validated on **San
Francisco real road networks + travel times**; near-optimal quality; can answer "how many vehicles
to hit an evacuation quota within time T."

## Key facts (as-published)
- Motivating real events cited in §1: Jan-2025 LA wildfires (30 direct / up to 440 linked deaths,
  >16,000 structures, >150,000 under evacuation notice); **May-2025 Blatten, Switzerland — 300
  villagers evacuated PRECAUTIONARILY on 19 May before a landslide destroyed the town on 28 May**
  (>130 buildings buried); Campi Flegrei (Naples) mass-evacuation planning (up to 135,000).
- BEOP generalizes Bus Evacuation Problem to the orienteering case where you CAN'T reach every point
  in time → maximize people moved. Stochastic demand/travel-time extensions sketched; MILP as oracle.

- TRINETRA's output is a **people-at-risk estimate + alert**; the natural DOWNSTREAM consumer is an
  **evacuation-routing** decision. GREAT-EER is the closest prior art for the routing layer and shows
  it's tractable in <1 s with a bounded-gap guarantee — useful for the "what does the alert trigger"

- MILP-as-oracle + bounded-gap is a good EVAL pattern: our downstream decisions should be benchmarked
