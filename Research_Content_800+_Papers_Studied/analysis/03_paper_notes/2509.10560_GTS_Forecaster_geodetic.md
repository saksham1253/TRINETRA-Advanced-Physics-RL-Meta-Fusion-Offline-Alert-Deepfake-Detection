## Content (own words)
- Contribution: open-source Python package for forecasting GEODETIC time series — GNSS
  positions, satellite-altimetry sea-surface-height (SSH), tide-gauge (TG) records — stated to
  enhance EARLY-WARNING systems + hazard mitigation (earthquakes, landslides, storm surge,
  sea level).
- Problem framing: geodetic series are NONLINEAR, NON-STATIONARY, and INCOMPLETE (gaps);
  classic models fail to capture long-term dependencies + spatiotemporal dynamics.
- Methods bundled: kernel attention networks (KAN), GNN-based gated recurrent units (GNNGRU),
  time-aware GNNs (TimeGNN). PLUS robust preprocessing: outlier detection + a REINFORCEMENT-
  LEARNING-based gap-filling algorithm, "Kalman-TransFusion Interpolation Framework" (KTIF).
- Verified numbers (Table 2, tide-gauge, R²): LSTM 0.84 (long) → LSTM_ekan 0.88 (KAN-enhanced
  beats plain LSTM); short-term LSTM_ekan R²=0.94. KAN-optimized + MLP models each win
  different horizons (KAN longer-term, LSTM/BiLSTM short-term).
