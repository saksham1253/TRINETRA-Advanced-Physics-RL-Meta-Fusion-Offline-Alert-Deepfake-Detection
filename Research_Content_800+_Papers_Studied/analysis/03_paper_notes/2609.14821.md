## What it is
A framework that makes uncertainty quantification **DECISION-ORIENTED** rather than merely statistically
calibrated: it tunes the UQ to the actual COST of downstream decisions, for Earth-system spatiotemporal
foundation models. Pipeline:
- **Probabilistic spatiotemporal foundation model** produces a predictive distribution (not a point).
- **Decision Risk Adapter:** maps the predictive distribution to the loss/utility of the *decision* that
  will be taken, not just forecast error.
- **Utility-Aware Calibration:** calibrates so that coverage holds on the quantity that matters for the
  decision (the "action loss"), not only on the raw prediction. Introduces an **action-loss coverage**
  notion distinct from predictive coverage.
- **Risk-Aware Decision Rule + optimization:** chooses the action to control risk under asymmetric,
  high-impact losses.
- Evaluated across THREE decision tasks, including **extreme-precipitation warning**.

## Verified numbers (§5 / Table 1, read directly)
- Raises **expected utility from 0.684 → 0.763 (+11.6%)**.
- **Predictive coverage 90.4% and action-loss coverage 90.2%** — both near the 90% target.
- Baselines fall short on the DECISION quantity: **Forecast Conformal reaches only 84.9% action-loss
  coverage** despite reliable *predictive* coverage; **Conformal Risk Control reaches 89.6%** action-loss
  but is weaker on regret and utility.
- For **extreme-precipitation warning, reduces regret by 23.4%** — the gain is largest precisely under
  asymmetric high-impact losses.
- Forecast-quality table: Point Forecast / Sample Risk RMSE ≈ 0.842; Sample Risk CRPS ≈ 0.486, predictive
  coverage 84.7% (baseline, before their calibration).

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (this is nearly our alarm-UQ thesis, published)
1. **The single closest published statement of our cost-sensitive-alarm doctrine.** TRINETRA's alarm
   policy is explicitly cost-loss driven (miss=−100, FA=−5/−1; Bayes threshold p*=C_FA/(C_FA+C_miss)=
   0.0476). This paper formalizes exactly that: calibrate/decide for the *decision loss*, and shows that
   ordinary conformal (calibrated on prediction) UNDER-covers the action loss (84.9% vs 90% target). →
   **Strong CR-04 corroboration AND a sharpening of it:** our per-zone conformal must target ACTION-LOSS
   coverage, not just predictive coverage. This is a concrete design upgrade for the GMF conformal layer.
2. **"Action-loss coverage ≠ predictive coverage" is a finding we must bake in.** It means a nominally
   90%-calibrated interval can still be miscalibrated for the alarm decision. Add to the UQ spec: report
   BOTH predictive and action-loss coverage; the alarm shield is tuned to action-loss coverage.
3. **Extreme-precipitation warning + 23.4% regret reduction** is directly our WHEN-layer (IMERG precip →
   cloudburst/flash-flood warning). Evidence that decision-oriented UQ helps *most* under asymmetric
   high-impact losses — exactly the life-safety regime (miss ≫ false-alarm cost).
4. **Built on a spatiotemporal FOUNDATION MODEL** — aligns with CR-01 (EO-FM encoder) + the forecast-
   residual anomaly stage; shows the FM→probabilistic-forecast→decision-adapter stack we are converging on.
5. **Utility/regret as the top-line metric** (not RMSE/CRPS alone) validates our Rule-16 process target:
   beat baselines on a *cost-loss* / decision metric under honest evaluation, not on accuracy alone.
6. **Honest baseline comparison** (Forecast Conformal, Conformal Risk Control both shown, both beaten on
   the decision metric) is the kind of fair-comparison discipline (Thread H) we require.
