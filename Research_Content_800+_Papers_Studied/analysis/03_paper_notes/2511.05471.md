## What it is
A **satellite-only** precipitation nowcasting model (TUPANN) trained on **GOES-16 RRQPE**, built for
places that LACK dense weather-radar networks — "limiting operational value in places most exposed to
climate extremes." Instead of a black-box video predictor, it **decomposes the forecast into physically
meaningful components:**
- **Variational encoder–decoder (VED)** infers MOTION and INTENSITY fields from recent imagery under
  **optical-flow supervision**.
- **Lead-time-conditioned MaxViT** evolves the latent; a **fixed differentiable advection/WARP operator
  (following NowcastNet)** reconstructs future frames from predicted motion+intensity fields:
  X̂_k = warp(v̂_{k−1→k}, ŝ_{k−1→k}, X̂_{k−1}).
- Validated against **IMERG** (the exact global satellite-precip product TRINETRA plans to use) across
  multiple regions; claims to transfer across regions / to extreme events better than SOTA baselines.

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (precipitation input layer, Himalaya-no-radar, physics-decomposition doctrine)
- **Addresses our EXACT constraint:** the Himalaya has sparse/absent weather radar; our rainfall input
  is satellite (GPM IMERG) + reanalysis. TUPANN demonstrates credible SHORT-TERM precip nowcasting from
  SATELLITE ALONE, and is explicitly validated on IMERG — it is a concrete candidate architecture (or
  at least design pattern) for the rainfall-forecast front-end that feeds our I–D thresholds (Caine
  I=a·D⁻ᵇ) and the TRIGRS/Iverson infiltration gate.
- **Reinforces the physics-decomposition doctrine** that recurs across our corpus (PINN FoS 2610.04828,
  physics-feature water-level 2511.20936, NowcastNet lineage): don't predict pixels directly — predict
  **physically meaningful latent fields (motion, intensity)** and reconstruct via a **fixed
  differentiable physics operator (advection/warp)**. This is the "physics as structure/gate, learner
  for residual" principle applied to nowcasting. Strong template for how we should structure our own
  rainfall predictor rather than a raw ConvLSTM.
- **Lead-time conditioning** is a clean, reusable mechanism for producing a FORECAST HORIZON set — maps
  onto our multi-horizon lead-time outputs and the per-horizon uncertainty widening (ties to TTsF
  onset-uncertainty lesson from 2203.13313 and the conformal interval work).
- **Transfer / cross-region generalization** is the headline — relevant to our spatial-CV concern (we
  must generalize across Uttarakhand↔Himachal zones). Pairs with REE-TTT (2601.01605, test-time
  training for cross-regional extreme precip — queued) as the "adaptation under distribution shift"
  options for the rainfall front-end.

## Caveats / honesty
- Trained/validated on GOES-16 (Americas) + IMERG; Himalayan transfer is PLAUSIBLE (IMERG is global)
  but UNVERIFIED for our terrain/monsoon regime — must be re-validated on Himalayan IMERG with spatial
  CV before any reliance; "outperforms SOTA / transfers to extremes" are the authors' claims on their
  regions.
- Nowcasting = short lead (minutes–few hours); it feeds, but does not replace, the longer-range
  reanalysis/forecast inputs; and precip nowcast skill for convective CLOUDBURSTS is intrinsically
  limited (do not overpromise cloudburst lead time — Rule 16/19).
