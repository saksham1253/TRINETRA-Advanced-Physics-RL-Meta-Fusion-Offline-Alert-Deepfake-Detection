## What it is
An **invariance-based self-distillation** (DINO-style momentum teacher/student) pretraining paradigm
built specifically for **time series**, using **wavelet (time-frequency) augmentations** instead of
vision-style spatial crops.
- Insight: next-token / reconstruction SSL "wastes capacity on high-frequency point-wise noise";
  spatial crops shift the timing of repeating cycles and distort signals → bad for temporal data.
- WinoTS builds **easy/hard multi-scale structural views** via discrete wavelet decomposition +
  multi-resolution basis randomization; teacher sees easy views, student sees easy+hard.
- **Architecture-agnostic** (drops into existing TS backbones with no structural change).

## Results (as published)
- Beats SOTA baselines on **long-term forecasting, cross-domain zero-shot transfer, AND unsupervised
  anomaly detection**.
- **Linear probing on frozen WinoTS representations frequently SURPASSES fully supervised models
  trained from scratch** — a strong "good SSL features > bespoke supervised" result.
- Systematic ablations: gains across multiple TS backbones; time-frequency transforms are a principled
  alternative to vision-style augmentations.

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (CR-01 encoder + WS8 TSAD + SSL recipe)
- **Our ground-sensor / InSAR-timeseries branch is exactly continuous-valued temporal data with strong
  cyclicity** (diurnal/seasonal rain, snowmelt, tidal-like deformation cycles). WinoTS's core warning —
  **don't use vision-style crops/jitter on temporal signals; use time-frequency views** — is a direct
  design rule for our SSL pretraining of the sensor/InSAR encoder.
- Links CR-01 (encoder trade study): for the TEMPORAL modality, the right SSL objective is
  invariance-based self-distillation with wavelet views, not reconstruction — complements 2606.13896
  (objective must match task) and SSL4EO-S12 (knee ~750K patches) from the IMAGE side.
- **Linear-probe > supervised** reinforces our data-scarce doctrine: pretrain SSL on abundant unlabeled
  Himalayan sensor streams, then a cheap linear/prototype head — ties to prototype-head (2610.06241)
  and the "normal as geometry" cluster.
- Unsupervised-AD gains make WinoTS a candidate PRETRAINING backbone for the TSAD anomaly branch
  (feeds the WS8 ensemble), with the NCL (2610.06453) caveat still enforced (guard residual collapse).
