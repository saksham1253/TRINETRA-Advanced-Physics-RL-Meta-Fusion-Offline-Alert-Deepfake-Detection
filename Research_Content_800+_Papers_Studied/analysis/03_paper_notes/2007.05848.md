## What it is
A mixed-methods (micro survey + content analysis / meso cross-platform / macro) study of
**#Verificado19S**, a citizen-led, multi-platform **verification** effort during Mexico's deadly
19 September 2017 earthquake. It characterizes how ordinary people **read, shared, and VERIFIED**
crisis information across Twitter → Facebook/Messenger/WhatsApp, and how a trusted citizen
organization bridged government and society to fight misinformation.

## Key observations (as published, qualitative)
- Distinct, tightly-coupled user activities: **"Sharing Needs," "Reading/Sharing/Verifying News,"
  "Mobilize people offline."** People used Twitter to triage WHERE help was needed, then moved to
  WhatsApp/Messenger to mobilize contacts into OFFLINE action.
- **A trusted intermediary organization** (neither pure-government nor pure-crowd) was central to
  keeping information relevant and credible — a bridge role.
- Design implications: mitigate misinformation DURING the event; keep information relevant/fresh;
  educate people about misinformation; empower trustworthy bridging organizations.

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (WS2/WS3 last-mile + crisis informatics + Rule 19)
- **Confirms the human-response layer is part of the system** (synthesis thread T3). An alert is only
  useful if people trust and act on it; this study shows trust flows through **known intermediaries**
  and **cross-platform verification**, not raw broadcast.
- **Design doctrine for TRINETRA's last-mile:** (1) route authoritative alerts through a TRUSTED
  bridging entity (SDMA / district authority), not anonymous channels; (2) expect and plan for
  misinformation to spread on WhatsApp during the event; (3) make alerts verifiable/traceable
  (signed CAP, ties RB-16) so citizen verifiers can corroborate; (4) the crowd layer is a
  mobilization + needs-triage channel, NOT a hazard-detection sensor (reinforces 2511.21364 /
  RB-09: social-media recall collapses when networks are down).
- **Global-South, real deadly event** → directly relevant evidence for an India deployment (SACHET +
  WhatsApp reality). Pairs with HaorFloodAlert's Bengali SMS/WhatsApp last-mile (2605.20167).
