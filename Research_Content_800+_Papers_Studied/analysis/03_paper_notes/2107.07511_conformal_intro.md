## Content (own words)
- Problem: black-box ML in high-risk settings needs rigorous uncertainty, but most UQ lacks
  guarantees.
- Core idea (re-implementable): CONFORMAL PREDICTION builds statistically rigorous prediction
  SETS/intervals around ANY pre-trained model's outputs, with a DISTRIBUTION-FREE, non-
  asymptotic coverage guarantee (e.g. the set contains the truth with user-specified prob
  1−α, say 90%), assuming only exchangeability. Recipe: on a held-out CALIBRATION set, define
  a nonconformity score, take the ceil((n+1)(1−α))/n empirical quantile q̂, then form the
  prediction set as all labels with score ≤ q̂. Variants read: Adaptive Prediction Sets (APS,
  include classes by cumulative softmax until > quantile), Conformalized Quantile Regression
  (wrap a quantile-regression model to get calibrated intervals), conformalizing scalar
  uncertainty, conformalizing Bayes. Extensions exist for distribution shift, time-series,
  outliers, models that abstain.
- Guarantee is MARGINAL (averaged over the calibration draw), not conditional — a key caveat.

## Relevance to us
- Component: WS6 (calibration/uncertainty) — this is THE anchor for TRINETRA's conformal
  layer. Takeaways: (1) we can wrap ANY hazard model (anomaly score, susceptibility,
  people-at-risk) to emit calibrated sets with a coverage guarantee the public/authorities
  can trust; (2) per-zone Mondrian conformal (our GMF design) = CONDITIONAL coverage by
  group, addressing the marginal-only caveat for high-relief vs valley zones; (3) exchange-
  ability is violated under distribution shift + time-series → must use the shift/time-series
  variants, and this is a real risk (R-14/CR on drift). Honest limit: marginal coverage ≠
  "90% safe for every slope".
