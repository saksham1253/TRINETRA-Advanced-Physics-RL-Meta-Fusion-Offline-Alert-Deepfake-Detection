## Problem & why it matters
Probabilistic short-range PRECIPITATION NOWCASTING — the primary near-term driver of flash-flood and
landslide triggering in our system. IRENE is a strong, honest, PROBABILISTIC + ENSEMBLE-CALIBRATED
nowcasting reference with a careful multi-metric evaluation and candid limits — a template for how to
build and HONESTLY evaluate our precipitation input layer.

## Core idea / method (re-implementable sketch)
- Encoder–forecaster on multi-scale Convolutional GRUs (ConvGRU); autoregressive forecaster;
  STOCHASTIC ENSEMBLE head → probabilistic 1 km / 5 min nowcasts over Italy.
- Trained on national radar composite (Italian Civil Protection, DPC).
- IMPORTANCE SAMPLING focuses training on precipitation-relevant events (rain is rare → re-weight).
- Primary loss: almost-fair CRPS (afCRPS, Lang et al. 2026) — corrects the positive bias of the
  finite-ensemble CRPS estimator (eq.8 energy form).
- Two variants: IRENE-GAN (sharper fields) and IRENE-GAN-RAPSD (spectral penalty on radially-averaged
  power spectral density to control fine-scale variance).

## Data / metrics
- Metrics: MAE (ensemble-mean), CRPS (probabilistic skill), RANK HISTOGRAM (calibration), RAPSD
  (spatial spectral realism). Benchmarks: STEPS (stochastic extrapolation) + DGMR (pretrained DL).

## Key results (as stated)
- All IRENE configs beat STEPS and DGMR on CRPS at EVERY lead time + rank histograms closer to uniform
  → better probabilistic skill AND ensemble calibration.
- BUT ensemble-mean MAE advantage is confined to the FIRST 90 min; beyond that the heavily-damped DGMR
  (and to a lesser extent STEPS) become competitive — honest admission of limited deterministic skill
  at long lead.
- Adversarial training removes the progressive loss of small-scale variance, but introduces EXCESS
  fine-scale power at long lead times that the spectral penalty only partially fixes → a documented
  trade-off, not a free win.

## Assumptions & limitations
- Italy radar domain; transfer to Himalaya (orographic extremes, sparse radar) untested.
- Nowcasting horizon short (skill good ~90 min; degrades after). Preprint.
