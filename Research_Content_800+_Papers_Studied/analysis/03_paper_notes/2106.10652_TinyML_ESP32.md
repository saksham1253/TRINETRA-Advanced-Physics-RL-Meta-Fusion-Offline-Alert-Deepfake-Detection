## Content (own words, from abstract)
- Problem/scope: ultra-low-power embedded devices (ESP32 / ESP32-Cam) + tinyML enable mass
  deployment of AI-powered IoT. Goal: analyze the SPEED of the Xtensa dual-core 32-bit LX6
  microprocessor (inside the ESP32) running a neural network in FEED-FORWARD mode.
- Method: feed-forward NNs with varying input sizes (9, 36, 144, 576) through 1 and 2
  hidden-layer networks; toolchain = Arduino IDE / MicroPython / TensorFlow Lite. Reports
  speed characteristics (specific timing numbers are in the PDF body — NOT read, not asserted).

## Relevance to us
