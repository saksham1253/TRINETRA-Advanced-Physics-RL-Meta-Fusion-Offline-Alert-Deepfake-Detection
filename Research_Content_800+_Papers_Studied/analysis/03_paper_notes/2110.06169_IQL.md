## Content (own words)
- Problem: offline RL must improve over the behavior policy while avoiding distributional-
  shift error. Most methods must query values of UNSEEN actions (then constrain/regularize).
- Core idea (re-implementable): NEVER evaluate out-of-sample actions. Treat the state value
  V(s) as a random variable over the dataset action distribution and estimate an upper
  EXPECTILE (asymmetric L2 / expectile regression) of it — this approximates
  max over in-support actions without querying a Q at unseen actions. Alternate: fit the
  expectile value function V_τ, back it up into Q, repeat; then extract the policy by
  advantage-weighted behavioral cloning (AWR-style). τ (expectile level) controls how
  optimistic/greedy; Lemma 2: V_τ monotonically increases in τ; Corollary 2.1: V_τ(s) stays
  bounded by the dataset-constrained optimal Q* (so it never leaves support).
- Claims: SOTA on D4RL; easy to implement; only one extra critic (asymmetric L2); supports
  online fine-tuning after offline init. (Exact numbers in pruned middle — not verified.)

## Relevance to us
- Component: WS5. IQL is the second offline-RL candidate. Takeaway: IQL's "never query OOD
  actions" is attractive for a SAFETY policy — the alarm policy cannot be seduced by an
  unobserved (state, alarm-level) pair. Simpler + lower-variance than CQL in many reports →
  strong one-/few-step baseline candidate, feeds CR-03.
- Code available (github.com/ikostrikov/implicit_q_learning) — good for a faithful baseline.
- Transfer hypothesis: expectile τ tuning on a tiny imbalanced disaster dataset is untested;
  AWR policy extraction behaviour under extreme class imbalance UNVALIDATED.
- confidence: med — method clear; experiments not fully seen.
