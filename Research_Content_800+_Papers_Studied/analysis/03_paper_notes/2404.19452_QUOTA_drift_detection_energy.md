## Content (own words)
- Problem: deployed ML decays via CONCEPT DRIFT; blind periodic RETRAINING wastes energy. Better
  tactic: MONITOR drift and retrain only when necessary — but the drift detectors THEMSELVES cost
  energy and accuracy, and that tradeoff was poorly quantified.
- Method: controlled full-factorial experiment, 420 combinations = 7 drift detectors (KSWIN,
  HDDM_W, HDDM_A, ADWIN, PageHinkley, DDM, EDDM) × 5 synthetic datasets × {abrupt, gradual} drift
  × 6 base classifiers. Measured energy consumption + drift-detection accuracy.
- Verified findings (from abstract + results table I read): three detector classes —
  (a) accuracy-at-energy-cost: KSWIN (best true-alarm %, 93.0% abrupt / 57.0% gradual, detection-
  closeness up to 0.998); (b) BALANCED low-medium energy + good accuracy: HDDM_W (~60% true-alarm)
  and ADWIN (~50-58%, but note ADWIN produced some MISSED alarms: 7 gradual / 12 abrupt);
  (c) very-low-energy but UNUSABLE due to poor accuracy: HDDM_A, PageHinkley, DDM, EDDM (true-
  alarm % in the single digits, EDDM as low as 0.7-1.3% with 56-73 missed drifts).

## What it CHANGED in our thinking (required for quota)
- Gives gap G4 (drift/OOD monitoring) a CONCRETE, defensible default: for TRINETRA's
  drift-triggered re-assurance (ties 2209.11632 quota + FM-07/FM-14), start with ADWIN or HDDM_W
  as the drift monitor — the "balanced" quadrant — rather than the energy-cheap-but-useless
  detectors. This is a design decision we previously had NO evidence for.
- Reframes drift monitoring as itself ENERGY-constrained — crucial because our ground segment is
  power-starved (2307.04016, 2010.14934, ACES 1909.01968). The cheapest detectors are a trap
  (they miss drift), so drift monitoring should run at the GATEWAY/cloud, not on the ₹2000 node.
  Adds a concrete note to the WS7 ↔ monitoring interface.
- Caution it injects: these detectors MISS a non-trivial fraction of drifts even at best (KSWIN
  misses 0 but is energy-heavy; ADWIN misses some). So drift detection is NOT a safety guarantee
  — it complements, never replaces, the physics gate + shield + human oversight (Rule 19). A
  missed drift must fail safe (toward MORE caution), consistent with miss-averse design.
