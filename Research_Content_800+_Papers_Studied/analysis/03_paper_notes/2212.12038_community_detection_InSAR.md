## Content (own words)
- Problem: slow-moving landslides creep (mm–m/yr) and can suddenly transition to RUNAWAY
  failure, often triggered by heavy precipitation (with time lags → failure even on a dry day).
  Predicting the creep→failure transition is hard.
- Method: a NETWORK-SCIENCE approach — multilayer MODULARITY OPTIMIZATION. Transform InSAR
  radar data into a spatially-embedded network: nodes = ground patches, edges connect nearest
  neighbors, layers = consecutive satellite transits. Each edge weighted by (local SLOPE from
  DEM, i.e. susceptibility) × (surface DEFORMATION from InSAR, i.e. current rheological state).
  Find strongly-connected communities. Study area: 2017 Mud Creek, Big Sur, CA.
- Findings: the method LOCATES Mud Creek AND nearby creeping-but-not-yet-failed landslides.
  They define "COMMUNITY PERSISTENCE" to quantify deformation patterns pre-failure, and find it
  RISES above baseline in the WEEKS leading up to Mud Creek's failure → candidate precursor.

## Relevance to us
- Component: WS1 InSAR precursor layer — directly relevant alternative/complement to pixel-wise
  anomaly scoring. Two takeaways: (1) FUSING slope (DEM susceptibility) × InSAR deformation into
  a single edge weight is exactly the physics-informed feature design TRINETRA wants (FS depends
  on slope; deformation = kinematic precursor) — a concrete, interpretable fusion of our static
  + dynamic layers; (2) "community persistence rising weeks before failure" is a spatiotemporal
  precursor metric that could feed the physics-gate / lead-time estimation, complementing the
  Fukuzono/Voight 1/v point metric (E-04) with a SPATIAL-pattern metric.
- Design value: network/community features are relatively interpretable + lightweight (fits
  CR-02 parsimony better than a big DL model) → candidate baseline feature for the InSAR anomaly
  head and for identifying WHICH slopes to watch.
- Honesty caveat: single site (Mud Creek, CA), slow-moving coastal landslide ≠ all our hazard
  types (rock-ice avalanche, GLOF, flash flood); "weeks" lead time is for creep-type failures,
  not sudden cloudburst debris flows → HYPOTHESIS for our setting (Rule 16). Numbers partially
  unread. confidence: med-high on method relevance; med on transfer.
- Feeds: WS1 InSAR feature design (slope×deformation edge weight), precursor-metric register
  (community persistence alongside 1/v), lead-time estimation, CR-02 (interpretable baseline).
