## Content (own words — abstract only)
- Problem: standard policy-gradient methods do one gradient update per sample (sample-
  inefficient); TRPO is complex to implement.
- Core idea (as stated in abstract): a new family of policy-gradient methods that alternate
  between sampling environment interaction and optimizing a "surrogate" objective by SGD,
  with a novel objective enabling MULTIPLE epochs of minibatch updates per batch. Keeps some
  TRPO trust-region benefits but is simpler, more general, better empirical sample
  complexity. Tested on simulated locomotion + Atari; outperforms other online PG methods.
- NOT verified here: the exact clipped-surrogate objective, clip epsilon, GAE details — those
  are in the unreadable body. Flagged for a full read if PPO becomes load-bearing (it is only
  the Stage-4 online fine-tuning option in the TRINETRA RL ladder).
