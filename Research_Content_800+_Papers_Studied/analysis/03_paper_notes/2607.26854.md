## What it is
A lightweight, portable **early-warning forecasting framework for DATA-SCARCE regions**. Core design is a
near-perfect analogue of TRINETRA's residual doctrine, applied to disease forecasting:
- **Mechanistic prior (ETSIR):** an Environmental Time-Series Susceptible-Infected-Recovered model
  provides a deterministic, physically-meaningful projection (their "physics").
- **NN-invariant gated residual correction:** a neural backbone learns a *gated residual* on top of the
  ETSIR prior — i.e. predict structure with the mechanistic model, learn only the residual with the net.
  "Node-invariant" = works across surveillance systems with different numbers of locations.
- **Multi-source training + two-scalar target adaptation:** train on data-RICH regions (Colombia,
  Nicaragua), then adapt to data-SCARCE targets (Mexico, Malaysia) by learning **only two global
  parameters (γ, β)** — extreme parameter-efficiency for transfer.
- **Conformal prediction** wraps the forecast for calibrated intervals.
- **Zero-shot foundation model:** when integrated with **TiRex** (a time-series foundation model applied
  ZERO-SHOT), TREA-Net achieves the lowest MAE across all targets.

## Verified numbers (main results table, read directly — MAE, lower=better)
Transfer to Malaysia / Mexico with only K=78 or K=104 weeks of target data:
- ETSIR alone (mechanistic only): Malaysia 1334.9 / 557.2 — poor alone.
- ARIMA: ~75–97. ETSIR-PINN: 48–75. Plain backbones: LSTM 34.7, NHiTS 28.4, TCN 36.6, PatchTST 49.3
  (Malaysia K=78).
- **TiRex (zero-shot foundation model): 23.8 / 26.6 (Malaysia), 54.9 / 60.1 (Mexico)** — strong zero-shot.
- TREA-Net improves its backbone in **9 of 10 transfer settings** (statistically significant); best MAE
  overall when integrated with TiRex.
- **Conformal prediction maintains empirical coverage while REDUCING 8-week interval width by 29.6% in
  Mexico** (verified from abstract + §4.6 head).

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (multi-axis, and it feeds the NEW P14Z phase)
1. **Fourth independent corroboration of the residual doctrine** (after MW-Nowcast, DRUM, Nowcasting-Nets):
   mechanistic/physics prior + neural *gated residual*. The GATED residual is especially relevant — it is
   essentially a 1-D version of our GMF "bounded residual policy" head. Evidence that gating the residual
   (not free-adding it) is the transferable, data-efficient choice.
2. **Direct template for TRINETRA's data-scarcity transfer.** Himalayan landslide/GLOF labels are scarce;
   this shows a concrete recipe — train on data-rich analogue regions, adapt with a tiny number of global
   scalars — that preserves the mechanistic backbone and needs almost no target data. The "two-scalar
   adaptation" is a cheap, auditable domain-adaptation pattern we can trial for cross-basin transfer.
3. **Conformal UQ with a real width reduction (29.6%) at maintained coverage** — concrete, same-as-ours
   CP evidence that calibrated intervals can be tightened without losing coverage. Strengthens CR-04 and
   the T5 "uncertainty first-class" thread.
4. **Zero-shot foundation-model forecasting (TiRex) is the single strongest baseline here** → direct,
   high-value input to the NEWLY-ADDED P14Z (zero-shot) phase: a time-series FM applied zero-shot beats
   trained backbones in a data-scarce early-warning setting. This is exactly the "the system can do
   zero-shot" capability the user flagged. **Park TiRex + this result for P14Z.** It also validates the
   P14Z design stance: zero-shot is powerful BUT best when a lightweight residual/adaptation layer corrects
   it locally (TREA-Net+TiRex > TiRex alone) — i.e. zero-shot as a prior, not a final answer.
5. **Gate interpretability section** (they inspect the learned gate) supports our requirement that the GMF
   residual/gate be auditable, not a black box.

## Caveats / honesty
- **Domain is DENGUE, not geohazard.** The ETSIR epidemic prior does NOT transfer; what transfers is the
  ARCHITECTURE (mechanistic prior + gated residual + tiny-parameter adaptation + CP + zero-shot FM
  backbone). Any TRINETRA use is a METHOD borrow, re-measured on our data (Rule 16). Do NOT cite dengue
  MAE numbers as geohazard evidence.
- MAE is scale-dependent and region-specific; the headline is the *relative* 9/10 improvement and the
  29.6% CP width reduction, not absolute error.
- Two-scalar adaptation is elegant but may underfit targets whose dynamics differ strongly from source
  regions; for geohazards the source/target physics gap could be larger than dengue-to-dengue.
- Conformal coverage here is marginal; local/conditional coverage under regime shift (our AFOCP concern,
  2511.15838) is not the same guarantee — a tight interval must still never suppress a physics-gated alarm.
