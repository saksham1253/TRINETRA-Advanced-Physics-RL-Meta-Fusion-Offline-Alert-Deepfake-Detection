## What it is
A **deep auto-encoder** tailored to **untangle ground deformation from atmospheric NOISE in InSAR time
series**, AUTONOMOUSLY — without prior knowledge of a fault's location or slip behaviour.
- **Problem it solves:** InSAR atmospheric propagation delays often EXCEED the ground deformation of
  interest, so classic InSAR needs time-consuming manual intervention + expert interpretation + a priori
  fault knowledge → precludes global/automatic monitoring.
- **Result:** on the North Anatolian Fault reaches **2 mm detection**, revealing a slow earthquake
  TWICE as extensive as previously recognized. Generalizes to inflation/deflation (Coso geothermal,
  California) with the SAME methodology.

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (InSAR precursor layer — the core SSL-encoder target)
- **This is foundational prior art for TRINETRA's Sentinel-1 InSAR precursor layer.** Our pipeline's
  front end is "SSL masked-modeling InSAR encoder → anomaly." Rouet-Leduc shows the KEY enabling move:
  **an autoencoder that separates genuine slow ground deformation from atmospheric noise WITHOUT labels
  or fault priors** — exactly the unsupervised/self-supervised regime we need (we have almost no
  labelled Himalayan slow-deformation ground truth).
- **Validates the "noise is the adversary, not the signal" framing for InSAR.** The dominant failure
  mode is atmospheric delay masquerading as deformation → our anomaly layer MUST model/remove
  atmospheric structure first (ties ERA5-Land tropospheric correction + the physics gate). This paper
  is the canonical citation that mm-scale deformation IS extractable from Sentinel-class InSAR once
  noise is untangled — supporting (with honest caveats) that a slow-creep precursor is physically
  observable at our sensor resolution.
- **2 mm sensitivity benchmark** gives a realistic expectation for the smallest slope-creep signal our
  InSAR layer could hope to flag — useful for setting detection-threshold and lead-time expectations
  (and for NOT overclaiming sub-mm).
- **Autoencoder-reconstruction = the anomaly primitive** we already use (Deep SVDD / forecast-residual
  / reconstruction error). This is the InSAR-specific instance; pairs with NCL (2610.06453) caution —
  a too-powerful FM can reconstruct anomalies too well, so the InSAR AE must be tuned to leave genuine
  deformation in the RESIDUAL, not absorb it. That tension is a concrete design note for WS1+WS8.
- Author lineage (Rouet-Leduc/Johnson/Hulbert) is the SAME group as the labquake-AE precursor work
  (2203.13313, #28) — consistent methodological thread: ML extracts weak geophysical precursor signals
  from noisy sensors.

## Caveats / honesty
- Tectonic SLOW-slip / volcanic inflation on major faults (North Anatolian, Coso) — NOT shallow
  rainfall-triggered landslide creep; the ATMOSPHERIC-noise-separation method + mm-scale-extraction
  CAPABILITY transfer to our setting, but landslide creep is shallower, more localized, and more
  rapidly rainfall-modulated — must be re-validated on Himalayan slope InSAR with ground truth before
  any reliance.
- 2 mm is for their LOS geometry / stacking depth / revisit; Himalayan layover-shadow in steep terrain
  will DEGRADE this (known Sentinel-1 limitation already flagged in our corpus) — do not assume 2 mm in
  gorges.
