## Life-safety framing (Rule 19)
Every failure mode is assessed for life-safety impact (missed alarm = death risk; false
alarm = trust erosion → future missed compliance). The system COMPLEMENTS, never replaces,
certified official warning systems, with human oversight on every real alert.
