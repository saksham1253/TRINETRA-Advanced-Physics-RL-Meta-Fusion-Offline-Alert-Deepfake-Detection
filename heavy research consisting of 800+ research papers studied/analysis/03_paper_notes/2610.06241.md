## What it is
**Prototype-only head adaptation** as a TinyML personalization primitive. A tiny 1-D CNN (**1,314
params, 72.6k MACs/beat**) is trained offline (MIT-BIH, inter-patient protocol), **frozen as a
feature extractor**, exported to a **PSoC 6 Cortex-M4F**. Personalization = computing **closed-form
class means (prototypes) in a 32-D embedding** — NO backprop, NO optimizer state, NO iterative
optimization, one forward pass per support beat.

## Numbers (as-published)
- Inter-patient macro-F1 improves 0.635/0.639/0.646 → **0.731/0.771/0.797 at 1/5/10-shot**,
  beating linear-SGD head fine-tuning at every shot count.
- On-device replay (18 one-shot episodes, PSoC 6 M4F): matches host macro-F1 (0.798), **11.39 ms/beat,
  5.2 KB flash, 22.2 KB SRAM**.
- Restricted variant updating only the normal-class prototype from passively buffered sinus beats:
  consistent +0.05 macro-F1, reducing annotation burden.

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (WS7 edge IoT nodes, ₹2000 budget)
- **Direct template for on-node adaptation under our ₹2000 constraint.** Our LoRa ground nodes + the
  River-Eye LSPIV nodes are microcontroller-class; the finding that **prototype (class-mean)
  adaptation needs NO backprop/optimizer** and runs in KB of SRAM/flash is exactly how we can let a
  node self-calibrate to its LOCAL baseline (soil-moisture/tilt/vibration/river signature) without a
  training loop on-device.
- **"Update only the normal-class prototype from passively buffered normal data"** maps perfectly to
  geohazard EWS: nodes see mostly NORMAL conditions; letting them refine their normal prototype
  passively (then flag deviations) is a cheap, label-free on-node anomaly primitive — and it pairs
  with the NCL insight (2610.06453) of constraining the normal subspace.
- Concrete feasibility datapoint: a useful ML head in **~5 KB flash / ~22 KB SRAM / ~11 ms** is well
  within ESP32-class budgets → supports the "intelligence at the edge, offline-first" architecture.
- NOTE: ECG hardware/medical domain — the TRANSFER (ECG→geohazard sensor streams) is our hypothesis;
  the on-device adaptation MECHANISM is the reusable part.
