## Content (own words)
- Problem: RL maximizes reward but gives NO safety guarantee during learning OR execution —
  unacceptable near humans / safety-critical control.
- Core idea (re-implementable): synthesize a SHIELD = a reactive system from a temporal-logic
  safety specification (safety fragment of linear temporal logic). Two placements:
  (1) PREEMPTIVE shield — before the agent acts, it provides the set of SAFE actions the agent
      may choose from (restricts action set each step);
  (2) POST-POSED shield — after the agent picks an action, the shield monitors and CORRECTS it
      only if it would violate the spec.
  Shield is built from a conservative (possibly finite-state) ABSTRACTION of the MDP + a
  deterministic safety automaton; the paper states requirements a shield must meet to PRESERVE
  the learner's convergence guarantees (minimal interference — only override when necessary).
- Guarantee: correctness against the spec holds during both learning and execution,
  regardless of convergence speed.

## Relevance to us
- Component: WS5 (RL alarm policy SAFETY SHIELD) — THE anchor for TRINETRA's hard overrides.
  Our shield rules (FS<1→Orange; tilt>5°/day→Red; 24h rain>150mm→Orange; GLOF sig→Red) are
  exactly a POST-POSED shield: let the learned policy choose normally, but override to a safe
  alarm level when a physics/sensor safety predicate fires. This paper gives the FORMAL
  backing (and the "minimal interference preserves convergence" argument) for that design,
  satisfying Rule 19 (human-safety, hard physics guardrails independent of the learned model).
- Takeaway: specify shield predicates in temporal logic; prove they only override when needed
  so RL can still learn. Preemptive form (mask unsafe alarm actions) may be cleaner for our
  discrete alarm MDP than post-posed.
- Transfer hypothesis: their control scenarios ≠ alarm-escalation MDP, but shielding is
  domain-general; our predicates are physics-derived, not learned → strong fit.
- confidence: HIGH (method + placement understood). Feeds safety-shield design (P7) + FM
  catalogue (shield as mitigation) + CR on RL-override authority.
