## Content (own words)
- Problem: floods cause massive deaths/damage, worst in high-population low-resource developing
  countries; India alone registers 1/5th of global flood deaths. Both EWS (water-level
  monitoring/forecast) and post-disaster PDSA (damage assessment) still largely MANUAL analysis
  of huge image/video volumes by first responders.
- Method: FloodTransformer — claimed first visual-transformer model to detect + segment FLOODED
  areas from AERIAL images at disaster sites. Proposes a custom metric "Flood Capacity (FC)" to
  measure spatial water-coverage extent for EWS + PDSA.
- Evidence (abstract): 0.93 mIoU on the SWOC Flood segmentation dataset, stated to outperform
  all compared methods; validated across unseen flood images from other sources (robustness).
- Notable framing (verified §1): cites that Indians given a flood warning are ~2× more likely
  to evacuate safely than those without notice — direct life-safety justification for EWS.

## Relevance to us

  The "Flood Capacity" extent metric is a candidate feature for the people-at-risk calculation.
- Life-safety doctrine (Rule 19): the "warning → 2× safe evacuation" statistic is exactly the
  benefit case for TRINETRA and belongs in design docs that justify the system's purpose.
