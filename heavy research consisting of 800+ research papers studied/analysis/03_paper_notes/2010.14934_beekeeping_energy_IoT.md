## Content (own words)
- Problem: autonomous outdoor IoT monitoring nodes deployed in "wild zones" live on a tight
  ENERGY BUDGET (batteries and/or energy harvesting). To design an energy-efficient,
  autonomous monitoring system you must first measure where the energy actually goes.
- Core idea (method, re-usable): take a FULLY AUTONOMOUS IoT framework (data-collecting node =
  environmental sensors + camera + Raspberry Pi + Arduino; plus a SOLAR energy-supplying node)
  and PROFILE energy consumption TASK-BY-TASK — sensing, imaging, compute, transmission — to
  identify the "highly pressured" (energy-hungry) areas. This profiling is the first step
  toward a sustainable design. (Specific per-task joule/watt numbers are in pruned tables —
  NOT read, not asserted.)

## Relevance to us
