## Content (own words — abstract only)
- Problem: extreme-flood forecasting suffers from peak-flow UNDER-estimation + high uncertainty.
- Method: DRUM = diffusion-based PROBABILISTIC deep-learning runoff model; generative AI applied to
  hydrology across representative CONUS basins.
- Reported results (ABSTRACT, NOT independently verified — D-008):
  - Enhances nowcasting skill for the TOP 0.1% of flows in 72.3% of studied basins.
  - Under operational scenarios, extends reliable lead time by ~1 full day for 20- and 50-yr floods.
  - **With MEASURED precipitation (an IDEAL condition), recall improves by 0.3–0.4 and the early-warning
    window extends by 2.3 days for 50-yr floods.**
  - Regional variation: precipitation-driven zones (eastern + northwestern US) gain most (3–7 days).
