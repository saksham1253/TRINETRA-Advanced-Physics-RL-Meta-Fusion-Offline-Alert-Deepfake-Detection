## Content (own words)
- Problem: unsupervised anomaly detectors have HIGH FALSE-POSITIVE rates because their
  assumed anomaly pattern mismatches reality + different users define anomalies differently.
  Human-in-the-loop verification (analyst checks top-ranked instances under a time budget)
  produces labels; greedy top-1 re-ranking is myopic — some low-ranked queries help more
  long-term.
- Core idea (re-implementable): learn a META-POLICY for QUERY SELECTION via deep RL (PPO)
  that explicitly optimizes the NUMBER OF ANOMALIES DISCOVERED over the whole querying
  budget (long-term reward), not greedy next-step. The meta-policy is trained across many
  datasets and then applied to NEW datasets with NO further tuning (meta/transfer). Uses PPO
  clipped surrogate (Eq.2) with value + entropy loss terms.
- Claim: on 24 benchmark datasets, Meta-AAD beats SOTA re-ranking + unsupervised baselines;
  trained meta-policy is transferable and balances long/short-term reward. (Tables pruned —
  not verified.)

## Relevance to us
- Component: GMF (gated meta-fusion) + WS8 anomaly + WS6 human-in-the-loop. STRONG: this is

  directly to our "which alerts to escalate to a human operator under limited responder
