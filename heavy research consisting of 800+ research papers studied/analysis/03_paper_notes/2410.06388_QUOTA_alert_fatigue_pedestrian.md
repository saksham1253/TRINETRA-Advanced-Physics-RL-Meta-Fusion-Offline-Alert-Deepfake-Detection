## Content (own words)
- Study: full-scale DRIVING SIMULATOR experiment comparing alert MODALITIES — audio-visual (AV),
  visual-tactile (VT), audio-visual-tactile (AVT) — vs no-alert baseline, for pedestrian-
  jaywalking events in an ADAS pedestrian-crossing alert. Focus: how drivers respond to
  modalities AND to FALSE ALARMS.
- Verified findings: vs no-warning baseline, multimodal alerts raised stopping distance +75% to
  +105% and stopped-count +96% to +157% across scenarios. VT gave the biggest stop-count gain
  (+11 vehicles); AV the biggest stopping-distance gain (+31 ft). BUT human-cost of false alarms:
  18 participants drove over-cautiously (slowing/checking when unnecessary); 7 said repeated
  FALSE ALARMS reduced trust → alert fatigue, some IGNORING subsequent warnings entirely; AVT
  (most intense) caused the highest stress + distraction; one driver fully stopped with NO
  pedestrian present.

## What it CHANGED in our thinking (required for quota)
- CONFIRMS and QUANTIFIES the cry-wolf failure (FM-09) with human-subject evidence: false alarms
  don't just annoy — they cause users to IGNORE later TRUE alarms (→ missed-evacuation = death
  risk) AND over-react harmfully. This is direct empirical support for treating false-alarm cost
  as more than a flat −5 RL reward: it has a DYNAMIC trust-erosion effect that compounds over
  time. ACTION: upgrade FM-09 mitigation + RL reward design (P15) to include a trust/habituation
  state, not just a per-event FA penalty.
- NEW design lesson — MODALITY MATTERS and MORE IS NOT BETTER: AVT (all three channels) produced
  the WORST stress/distraction despite more information. For TRINETRA's community alerting
  (sirens, SMS/CAP, app), this argues for CALIBRATED modality escalation tied to alert level
  (Rule 19 human-oversight), NOT maximal multimodal blasting. Adds a design note to the alerting/
  dissemination layer and to the people-at-risk→CAP routing (§3).
- Reinforces that EVALUATION of an EWS must measure HUMAN RESPONSE (trust, compliance, fatigue),
  not just ML detection metrics — feeds the §P1 "evaluation methodology for safety-critical EWS"
  deliverable and the key-questions doc (Q on evacuation/decision/value-of-warning).

## Honesty caveats
- Driving-simulator ADAS (seconds-scale, individual driver) ≠ community mountain-hazard warning
  (minutes-to-hours, population). The TRUST-EROSION MECHANISM transfers; the magnitudes do NOT.
  Numbers are theirs (Rule 16). confidence: high on the qualitative trust/fatigue mechanism;
  low on numeric transfer. Feeds FM-09, RL reward (P15), alerting-modality design, key questions.
