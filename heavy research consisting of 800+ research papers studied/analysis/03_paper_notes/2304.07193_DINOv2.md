## Content (own words)
- Problem: produce general-purpose visual features (foundation model) usable WITHOUT
  fine-tuning, at image AND pixel level, surpassing text-guided (OpenCLIP) features.
- Core idea (re-implementable at concept level): scale SELF-SUPERVISED learning (DINO/iBOT
  lineage — image-level + masked patch-level objectives) on a large AUTOMATICALLY-CURATED,
  deduplicated, diverse image dataset (a retrieval-based curation pipeline, not raw web
  scrape). Train a 1B-param ViT-g/14, then DISTILL into smaller ViT-L/B/S that beat
  from-scratch training. Many contributions are training-stability/efficiency tricks at scale.
- Evidence seen (ablation table): distilled ViT-L/14 beats scratch ViT-L/14 on all 12
  benchmarks (e.g. INet-1k 86.3 vs 84.5; Segmentation 73.3 vs 72.2) and approaches the
  ViT-g/14 topline — validates "distill a big SSL model into small ones."

## Relevance to us
- Component: WS1/WS8 (A2 EO foundation-model / SSL encoder). Takeaway: DINOv2 is the general-
  purpose-ViT contender in CR-01 (bespoke RS/SAR encoder vs general ViT). Its features work
  frozen (no fine-tuning) → attractive given our Kaggle compute limits; distillation path
  gives small edge-deployable encoders.
- CONTRADICTION (CR-01): DINOv2 argues general curated-SSL features transfer broadly; the
  bespoke-RS-FM side (2510.17014) argues SAR/InSAR needs domain-specific pretraining
  (feature reconstruction, not RGB pixels). Our InSAR is NOT natural RGB → transfer is a
  HYPOTHESIS; DINOv2-on-SAR likely needs adaptation. Resolve empirically (P14/P8).
- Transfer hypothesis: ImageNet-domain SSL features on Sentinel-1 interferograms / amplitude
  is UNVALIDATED; strong prior that raw DINOv2 underperforms a SAR-pretrained encoder on
  deformation tasks.
- confidence: high on method/benchmarks; low on direct InSAR transfer.
