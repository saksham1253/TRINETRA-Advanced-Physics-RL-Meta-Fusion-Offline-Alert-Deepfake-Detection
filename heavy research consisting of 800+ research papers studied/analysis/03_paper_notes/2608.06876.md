## What it is
A **weakly-supervised federated** framework for Video Anomaly Recognition (VAR, fine-grained, not just
binary) built on a frozen VLM. The core problem it solves: **semantic misalignment** — distributed
edge clients learn divergent notions of "normal" vs "abnormal," which breaks fine-grained federated
recognition.
- **Mechanism:** each client computes a local **normality prototype**; the server aggregates them into
  a **global prototype m_g** and redistributes it; clients **re-center both visual and textual features**
  on m_g so everyone operates in an aligned space. Only lightweight modules (vision-text-prompt +
  temporal) train locally; raw video never leaves the client.
- Evaluated under non-IID partitions, unseen domains, and novel anomaly classes; beats SOTA federated
  baselines with minimal communication; includes a convergence analysis.

## Why it matters to TRINETRA (WS4 CCTV + privacy + the "normal as geometry" theme)
- **Privacy-preserving CCTV/crowd layer.** TRINETRA's video layer sees sensitive footage (people,
  homes). FedVAR shows how to train a shared anomaly recognizer **without centralizing raw video** —
  essential for consent/ethics/law (flagged for expert review) and for the many independent CCTV
  owners across Uttarakhand/Himachal.
- **Reinforces the unifying "normal = geometry, align it" theme** now spanning FOUR reads:
  prototype-head (2610.06241), normal-subspace NCL (2610.06453), IsoMaxPlus distance-logits
  (2608.16654), and now a federated **global normality prototype** (2608.06876). → WS8 design doctrine:
  represent normality as a prototype/subspace; the federated twist is to AGREE on that geometry across
  nodes before flagging deviations. This directly fixes the real risk that each TRINETRA site/zone
  drifts to its own idiosyncratic "normal."
- Pairs with 2609.07455 (P2 federated VLM-verify): federated + cheap-local-scorer + shared-anchor is a
  coherent WS4 architecture.
